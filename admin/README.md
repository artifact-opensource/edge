## 🎯 STRATEGIC ROADMAPS (Source of Truth)

### **BOD_ROADMAP.md** - Board of Directors Strategic Roadmap
- **Purpose:** Board-level strategic oversight and governance
- **Scope:** 8-phase roadmap from formation (2025) to exit (2030+)
- **Contains:**
  - Strategic milestones with comprehensive dependencies
  - Funding requirements ($9.6M-15.8M total)
  - Board governance framework and approval thresholds
  - Cross-phase dependency matrix
  - Risk management and success criteria
- **Review:** Quarterly by Board
- **Classification:** Confidential - Board Level
- **Owner:** Board of Directors

### **CEO_EXECUTION_PLAN.md** - CEO Tactical Execution Plan
- **Purpose:** CEO's operational execution guide (synced with BOD roadmap)
- **Scope:** Detailed tactical breakdown of all strategic milestones
- **Contains:**
  - CEO directives with quick steps (minimal-word, actionable)
  - CEO-level dependencies (incorporates BOD deps + adds tactical deps)
  - Weekly execution rhythm aligned with ceo-weekly-sop.md
  - KPIs, risk management, and accountability metrics
  - Precise timelines and ownership assignments
- **Review:** Weekly (operational) / Monthly (strategic)
- **Classification:** Confidential - Executive
- **Owner:** Office of the CEO

**Critical:** These two documents **must remain synchronized**:
- Every BOD milestone cascades to CEO execution
- CEO incorporates all BOD dependencies + tactical dependencies
- Start/end dates are precisely aligned
- Weekly CEO reviews ensure board alignment
