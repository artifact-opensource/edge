WRITING GUIDELINES:
================================================================================

1. CLARITY: Use clear, concise language. Avoid jargon unless necessary.

2. STRUCTURE: Organize information logically with headings and bullet points.

3. TONE: Maintain professional, respectful tone appropriate to the subject.

4. BREVITY: Keep memos as brief as possible while covering all necessary points.

5. ACTION-ORIENTED: Clearly state what action is required and by whom.

6. COMPLETENESS: Include all information recipients need to understand and act.

7. ACCURACY: Verify all facts, figures, dates, and names before distribution.

8. PROOFREADING: Review for grammar, spelling, and formatting errors.
