## Islamabad AI/ML Infrastructure – Strategic Position

### Competitive Advantages
✓ **Fastest-growing data center hotspot** in Pakistan (18.70% CAGR)  
✓ **CPEC fiber corridor** — dual international connectivity + Western China access  
✓ **Climate efficiency** — 7% cooling cost advantage vs. Karachi  
✓ **2,000 MW surplus power** earmarked for AI clusters  
✓ **Government demand anchor** — National Data Center (100+ agencies)  
✓ **NASTP ALPHA STZ** (Rawalpindi) — AI & Cyber Security focused  
✓ **Low regulatory friction** — PSEB registration in 9 minutes for SECP entities  
✓ **Sector momentum** — 28% export growth H1 FY2024-25

### Operational Requirements Checklist
- [ ] PSEB Registration (portal.techdestination.com)
- [ ] SECP Company Registration
- [ ] PTA CVAS License/Registration (hosting & data services)
- [ ] PTA IP & VPN Registration
- [ ] PTA Type Approval (imported equipment)
- [ ] FBR NTN & Tax Compliance
- [ ] SBP Foreign Exchange Compliance
- [ ] PTA Security Audit (CTDISR standard)

### Risk Factors
⚠ **Power grid unreliability** — primary operational constraint  
⚠ **Talent scarcity** — 35K graduates vs. 100K sector need; 25% wage premium for certified engineers  
⚠ **STZ expansion paused** — new zone notifications halted (IMF conditionalities)  
⚠ **Tax incentive verification needed** — IT export income tax credit status post-June 2025
