### 1. ARC (Adaptive Resilience Chain)

**Repository:** https://github.com/Artifact-Virtual/ARC.git  
**Category:** Flagship, Blockchain  
**Status:** Active Development  
**Technology:** Blockchain, Identity, SBT (Soul-Bound Tokens)

**Description:**
ARC is Artifact Virtual's flagship blockchain platform focused on identity management and on-chain resilience. It implements Soul-Bound Tokens (SBT) for non-transferable identity credentials and incorporates Hybrid Type Theory for advanced blockchain architecture.

**Key Features:**
- Soul-Bound Token (SBT) identity system
- Hybrid Type Theory implementation
- On-chain resilience mechanisms
- Decentralized identity management
- Cryptographic verification systems

**Strategic Importance:** HIGH
- Core identity infrastructure for entire Artifact ecosystem

- Competitive differentiation in blockchain identity space

**Market Position:**
