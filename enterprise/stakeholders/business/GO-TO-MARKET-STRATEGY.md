# Go-to-Market Strategy

**Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited**  
**Market Entry & Customer Acquisition Strategy**

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** February 2026  
**Classification:** Confidential - Executive Leadership  
**Horizon:** 2026-2028 (Initial 3 years)

## Executive Summary

This document outlines Artifact Virtual's comprehensive go-to-market (GTM) strategy for entering and capturing market share across three geographic regions: Pakistan (physical operations), United States and European Union (virtual operations), and globally (cloud platform).

**Strategy Overview:**
- **Phase 1 (2026 Q1-Q2):** Pakistan market entry with physical infrastructure
- **Phase 2 (2026 Q2-Q4):** US/EU virtual operations launch
- **Phase 3 (2027+):** Global cloud platform scaling

**Target Metrics Year 1:**
- 25-35 customers acquired
- $750K ARR achieved
- $50K-300K MRR progression
- 3-4 reference customers in each segment

---

## 1. Market Segmentation

### 1.1 Geographic Segments

#### Pakistan Market
**Profile:**
- Total market: PKR 80-100B (~$280-350M USD)
- Growth rate: 15-20% CAGR
- Islamabad focus: 35-40% of national IT market
- Underserved with modern infrastructure

**Target Customers:**
- Banks and financial institutions
- Telecom operators
- Government ministries and agencies
- Universities and research institutions
- Tech startups (Series A+)

**Entry Strategy:**
- Physical data center presence
- Local sales team and support
- Partnership with system integrators
- Direct relationships with decision makers

#### US/EU Market
**Profile:**
- Combined market: $100B+
- AI/ML segment growth: 25-30% CAGR
- Remote services model feasible
- Higher price points acceptable

**Target Customers:**
- AI/ML startups (Seed to Series B)
- Research universities and labs
- Fintech companies
- SaaS companies requiring ML capabilities

**Entry Strategy:**
- Virtual operations (no physical presence initially)
- Inbound marketing and digital channels
- Open-source community engagement
- Remote-first service delivery

### 1.2 Customer Segments

#### Segment A: Pakistani Enterprises
**Characteristics:**
- 50-500 employees
- $10M-100M annual revenue
- Need: Reliable, cost-effective infrastructure
- Decision makers: CTO, IT Manager
- Sales cycle: 3-6 months
- Annual value: $10K-50K

**Pain Points:**
- Unreliable power and connectivity
- Expensive international hosting
- Lack of local technical support
- Compliance with local data regulations

**Value Proposition:**
- Local presence with international standards
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- 24/7 local support team
- Cost-effective pricing (30-40% below international)

#### Segment B: AI/ML Startups (US/EU)
**Characteristics:**
- 5-50 employees
- Seed to Series B funded
- Need: Flexible, scalable AI/ML infrastructure
- Decision makers: CTO, VP Engineering
- Sales cycle: 1-3 months
- Annual value: $20K-100K

**Pain Points:**
- High cost of hyperscaler GPU instances
- Complex pricing and vendor lock-in
- Generic infrastructure not optimized for ML
- Difficulty getting technical support

**Value Proposition:**
- 60-70% cost savings vs. hyperscalers
- AI/ML optimized infrastructure
- Specialized technical expertise
- Flexible, transparent pricing

#### Segment C: Research Institutions
**Characteristics:**
- Universities, research labs
- Grant-funded or institutional budget
- Need: HPC/GPU computing for research
- Decision makers: Lab directors, research admins
- Sales cycle: 3-9 months (grant cycles)
- Annual value: $50K-200K

**Pain Points:**
- Limited budgets for commercial infrastructure
- Need flexible, on-demand compute
- Complex technical requirements
- Procurement processes

**Value Proposition:**
- Academic pricing programs
- High-performance computing expertise
- Flexible billing (monthly, grant-based)
- Research collaboration opportunities

## 2. Market Entry Strategy

### 2.1 Phase 1: Pakistan Market (Q1-Q2 2026)

**Objective:** Establish local presence, acquire 10-15 initial customers, achieve $50K-100K MRR

**Timeline:** January - June 2026

**Key Activities:**

**Month 1-2 (Jan-Feb):**
- Data center facility lease finalized
- Initial infrastructure deployment
- Sales team hiring (2 sales reps)
- Marketing materials development
- Website and collateral launch

**Month 3-4 (Mar-Apr):**
- Data center Phase 1 operational
- First customer pilots initiated (3-5 companies)
- Direct outreach to warm leads (network)
- Attendance at Pakistan tech events
- LinkedIn campaign for local enterprises

**Month 5-6 (May-Jun):**
- Convert pilots to paying customers
- Reference customer case studies
- Expand outreach to cold prospects
- Partnership discussions with system integrators
- Target: 10-15 customers, $50K-100K MRR

**Tactics:**

1. **Network Leverage:**
   - Personal introductions from founders and advisors
   - Board member connections
   - Industry association memberships

2. **Direct Sales:**
   - Targeted account list (50 tier-1 prospects)
   - Personalized outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone)
   - Executive meetings and facility tours
   - Custom proposals and pilots

3. **Local Presence:**
   - Office in Islamabad IT district
   - Visible participation in tech community
   - Sponsorship of local events
   - Media coverage in tech publications

4. **Competitive Displacement:**
   - Target customers unhappy with current providers
   - Offer migration assistance and incentives
   - Comparative pricing and feature analysis

**Success Metrics:**
- 50+ sales meetings conducted
- 15-20 proposals delivered
- 10-15 customers signed
- $50K-100K MRR achieved
- 3+ reference customers

## 3. Sales Strategy

### 3.1 Sales Process

**Stage 1: Lead Generation**
- Inbound: Website forms, GitHub, content downloads
- Outbound: LinkedIn, email, cold calling
- Referrals: Customer and partner introductions
- Events: Conferences, webinars, meetups

**Stage 2: Qualification (BANT)**
- **Budget:** $10K-50K+ annual IT infrastructure budget
- **Authority:** Access to CTO, VP Eng, or IT Director
- **Need:** Clear pain point we can solve
- **Timeline:** Decision within 1-6 months

**Stage 3: Discovery**
- Technical requirements gathering
- Current infrastructure assessment
- Pain point deep dive
- Success criteria definition
- Stakeholder mapping

**Stage 4: Proposal & Demo**
- Custom solution design
- Technical demonstration
- Pricing and commercial terms
- ROI analysis
- Security and compliance review

**Stage 5: Proof of Value**
- Pilot or trial period (1-3 months)
- Technical onboarding
- Success metrics tracking
- Executive check-ins
- Reference customer calls

**Stage 6: Negotiation & Close**
- Contract finalization
- Legal and procurement coordination
- Executive alignment
- Signature and activation
- Onboarding kickoff

**Stage 7: Expansion**
- Quarterly business reviews
- Additional use case identification
- Upsell and cross-sell opportunities
- Referral requests

### 3.2 Sales Team Structure

**Year 1 (2026):**
- VP Sales / CMO (1) - Leadership and strategy
- Inside Sales Reps (2) - Pakistan market, SMB accounts
- Sales Engineer (1) - Technical pre-sales support
- SDR (1) - Lead qualification and outreach (H2 2026)

**Year 2 (2027):**
- VP Sales (1)
- Sales Managers (2) - Pakistan, International
- Account Executives (5) - Enterprise accounts
- Inside Sales (3) - SMB accounts
- Sales Engineers (2) - Technical support
- SDRs (3) - Lead generation

**Year 3 (2028):**
- VP Sales (1)
- Regional Sales Directors (3) - Asia, Americas, EMEA
- Account Executives (10+)
- Inside Sales (5+)
- Sales Engineers (4)
- SDRs (5+)

### 3.3 Sales Compensation

**Base + Variable Structure:**
- AEs: 60% base, 40% variable (OTE: $80K-120K)
- Inside Sales: 70% base, 30% variable (OTE: $50K-80K)
- SEs: 80% base, 20% variable (OTE: $90K-130K)
- SDRs: 65% base, 35% variable (OTE: $40K-60K)

**Commission Structure:**
- New business: 10-15% of first-year ACV
- Renewals: 5% of ACV
- Upsells: 10% of incremental ACV
- Accelerators for over-quota performance

**SPIFFs (Special Incentives):**
- First deal in new vertical/region
- Largest deal of quarter
- Fastest time-to-close
- Reference customer acquisition

### 3.4 Sales Enablement

**Tools:**
- CRM: HubSpot or Salesforce
- Sales engagement: Outreach.io or SalesLoft
- Proposal software: PandaDoc or Proposify

**Training:**

**Content:**

## 7. Competitive Strategy

### 7.1 Competitive Positioning

**vs. Hyperscalers (AWS, GCP, Azure):**
- **Our Advantage:** 60-70% cost savings, specialized AI/ML expertise, personalized service
- **Messaging:** "Enterprise quality without enterprise pricing"
- **Target:** Cost-conscious customers, specialized AI/ML needs

**vs. Specialized AI Infrastructure (CoreWeave, Lambda):**
- **Our Advantage:** Geographic diversity, broader service portfolio, custom solutions
- **Messaging:** "Global reach with local presence"
- **Target:** International customers, Pakistan market, integrated solutions

**vs. Local Providers (Pakistan):**
- **Our Advantage:** Modern infrastructure, AI/ML expertise, international standards
- **Messaging:** "International quality, local presence"
- **Target:** Enterprises requiring reliability and innovation

### 7.2 Competitive Intelligence

**Activities:**
- Regular competitor monitoring (websites, pricing, announcements)
- Win/loss analysis with customers
- Battle cards for sales team
- Product feature comparison matrices
- Pricing benchmarking quarterly

**Response Strategy:**
- Fast follower on commodity features
- Differentiation on novel tech and service
- Compete on value, not just price
- Build deep customer relationships
