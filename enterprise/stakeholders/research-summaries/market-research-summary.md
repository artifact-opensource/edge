### Market Size & Growth
- **Current Market:** PKR 80-100 billion ($280-350M USD)
- **CAGR:** 15-20% (highest growth rate)
- **IT Export:** $2.6B (2025), growing 25% annually
- **Digital Pakistan:** Government initiative driving demand

### Islamabad Region Analysis
- **Market Share:** 35-40% of national IT market
- **Government:** Largest customer segment (30-35%)
- **Enterprises:** Growing rapidly (25-30%)
- **Startups:** Emerging tech ecosystem (15-20%)
- **Education:** Universities and research (10-15%)

### Infrastructure Gap Analysis
**Current State:**
- Limited Tier III/IV data center capacity
- Frequent power outages (backup required)
- Limited international connectivity
- Shortage of specialized ML/AI infrastructure

**Opportunity Size:**
- Only 5-7 modern data centers nationwide
- Government digitalization: $500M-1B investment
- Private sector digital transformation accelerating
- Startup ecosystem growing 30-40% annually

- **Real Estate:** 80-85% lower than major tech hubs
- **Power Costs:** Competitive with backup systems

### Market Dynamics
**Drivers:**
- Government Digital Pakistan initiative
- 5G rollout and connectivity improvements
- Growing IT export industry
- Emerging startup ecosystem
- Increasing cloud adoption

**Challenges:**
- Power reliability concerns
- Limited international bandwidth
- Currency fluctuation risks
- Talent retention
- Infrastructure gaps
