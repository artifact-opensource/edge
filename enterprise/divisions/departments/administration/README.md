## 📝 Usage Instructions

1. **Select Template:** Choose the appropriate template for your need
2. **Customize:** Fill in required fields (highlighted in [BRACKETS])
3. **Review:** Ensure all information is accurate and complete
4. **Approval:** Get necessary approvals as per company policy
5. **Store:** Save completed documents in appropriate department folders
6. **Encrypt:** Encrypt confidential documents before committing (see `docs/DECRYPTING_FILES.md`)
