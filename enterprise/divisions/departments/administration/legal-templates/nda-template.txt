================================================================================

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party 
    to the other, whether orally, in writing, or in any other form, including:

    a) Technical information: algorithms, code, designs, specifications, 
       methodologies, processes, know-how
    
    b) Business information: strategies, plans, financial data, customer 
       lists, vendor information, pricing
    
    c) Proprietary information: trade secrets, inventions, research, 
       development plans
    
    d) Any information marked "CONFIDENTIAL" or "PROPRIETARY"
    
    e) Information that reasonably should be understood as confidential

1.2 Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
    
    a) Was publicly known at time of disclosure
    b) Becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement  
    c) Was rightfully in possession prior to disclosure
    d) Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
    e) Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction

================================================================================

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

2.1 The Recipient shall:
    
    a) Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential
    b) Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent
    c) Use same degree of care as with own confidential information 
       (but no less than reasonable care)
    d) Use Confidential Information only for the stated Purpose
    e) Limit access to employees/agents with legitimate need to know
    f) Ensure those with access are bound by similar obligations

2.2 The Recipient shall NOT:
    
    a) Copy or reproduce without written permission
    b) Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble
    c) Use for own benefit or third-party benefit
    d) Remove confidentiality markings or notices

================================================================================

3. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES

3.1 Disclosure is permitted when:
    
    a) Required by law, regulation, or court order (with prior notice 
       to Disclosing Party if legally permissible)
    
    b) Necessary for Purpose, to authorized persons under confidentiality 
       obligations

================================================================================

4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

4.1 Upon request or termination of discussions:
    
    a) Return all Confidential Information (including copies)
    b) OR Destroy all Confidential Information with written certification
    c) Delete all electronic copies
    d) Obligations of confidentiality continue

================================================================================

5. NO LICENSE OR TRANSFER

5.1 This Agreement does NOT:
    
    a) Grant any license or right to Confidential Information
    b) Transfer any intellectual property rights
    c) Create any obligation to proceed with business relationship
    d) Guarantee accuracy or completeness of information

================================================================================

6. REMEDIES

6.1 Breach may cause irreparable harm
6.2 Disclosing Party entitled to:
    
    a) Injunctive relief (without posting bond)
    b) Monetary damages
    c) Attorney's fees and costs
    d) Any other remedies available at law or equity
