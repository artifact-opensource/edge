## 1. Contract Management

### 1.1 Contract Review

**Review Process:**
1. Request submitted
2. Assign to legal counsel
3. Review and markup
4. Negotiation support
5. Final approval
6. Execution coordination

**Review Timeline:**
| Contract Type | SLA |
|---------------|-----|
| Standard (templates) | 2 days |
| Modified terms | 5 days |
| Complex/custom | 10 days |

### 1.2 Contract Approval

**Approval Matrix:**
| Contract Value | Approver |
|----------------|----------|
| <$10,000 | Manager + Legal |
| $10,000-$100,000 | Director + Legal + Finance |
| >$100,000 | VP + Legal + CFO |

### 1.3 Contract Repository

**Repository Requirements:**
- Centralized storage
- Version tracking
- Expiry alerts
- Searchable metadata
- Access controls
