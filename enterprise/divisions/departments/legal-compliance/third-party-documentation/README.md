# Third-Party Documentation Package

**Purpose:** Single, externally consumable bundle to satisfy common agency, partner, due diligence, and marketing vendor requirements with minimal back-and-forth.

## Core Pack (Universal)
- Corporate profile & org chart
- Certificate of incorporation / business registration
- Memorandum & Articles of Association (or equivalent)
- Board resolutions / signing authority matrix
- Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) declaration
- Tax registration certificates (e.g., FBR, VAT/GST)
- Insurance certificates (professional liability, cyber, general)
- Audited financial statements (last 2-3 years) or management accounts
- Bank reference letter (if requested)
- Primary points of contact (legal, finance, security)

## Compliance & Risk
- Information security policy summary
- Data privacy policy & data handling statement
- Incident response plan overview
- Business continuity & disaster recovery summary
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) template
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) / cross-border transfer basis
- Risk register summary (non-confidential)
- Compliance attestations (ISO 27001/9001, SOC 2, GDPR readiness) if available

## Commercial & Contracting
- Master Services Agreement (MSA) template
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) templates (mutual + one-way)
- Standard Statement of Work (SOW) template
- Service Description / Product Fact Sheets
- Pricing & discounting policy summary (external-safe)
- Warranty, acceptance, and SLA summary (response/resolution targets)
- Payment terms, invoicing process, and banking details (sanitized)

## Marketing & Brand
- Brand guidelines (logo usage, colors, typography)
- Boilerplate company description (short/long)
- Product one-pagers and case studies
- Reference clients / testimonials (approved)
- Press kit (logos, founder bios, headshots)
- Social media handles and official links

## Regulatory & Sector-Specific (as applicable)
- Sector licenses or approvals (e.g., fintech, telecom, health)
- Export control / sanctions compliance statement
- Data residency statement (hosting regions, subprocessors)
- Accessibility conformance summary (e.g., WCAG)
- Environmental, Social, Governance (ESG) policy snapshot

## Submission & Currency
- Document classification and external-safe redactions applied
- Validity periods and renewal cadence tracked
- Single owner for updates; quarterly review cycle
