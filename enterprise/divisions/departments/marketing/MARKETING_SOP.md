## 1. Brand Management

### 1.1 Brand Guidelines

**Brand Elements:**
- Logo usage
- Color palette
- Typography
- Imagery style
- Voice and tone

**Brand Review Process:**
1. All external materials reviewed
2. Brand checklist verification
3. Marketing approval
4. Legal review (if claims)
5. Final sign-off

### 1.2 Asset Management

**Asset Library:**
- Centralized repository (Notion/Drive)
- Version control
- Usage rights tracked
- Regular audits

**Asset Request:**
1. Submit request with specifications
2. Check existing library
3. Create/modify if needed
4. Approve and publish
5. Update library

## 5. Lead Generation

### 5.1 Lead Capture

**Lead Sources:**
- Website forms
- Content downloads
- Event registrations
- Webinar signups
- Paid advertising
- Referrals

**Lead Capture Requirements:**
- Name
- Email
- Company
- Title
- Source tracking

### 5.2 Lead Qualification

**Lead Scoring:**
| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Title (decision maker) | +20 |
| Company size (target) | +15 |
| Engagement (downloads) | +10 |
| Website visits | +5 |

**MQL Criteria:**
- Score >50 points
- Valid contact info
- Target profile match

### 5.3 Lead Handoff

**Handoff Process:**
1. Lead reaches MQL threshold
2. Enrichment (if needed)
3. Assignment to sales
4. Notification
5. Tracking

**SLA:**
- Marketing to Sales handoff: <4 hours
- Sales follow-up: <24 hours
