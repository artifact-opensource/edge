## Executive Summary

Artifact Virtual's financial model is built on a three-phase expansion strategy: **Local Operations** (Pakistan, Months 1-18), **Virtual Operations** (US/EU, Months 7-24), and **Cloud Operations** (Global, Months 19-36). This document provides detailed revenue projections, income breakdowns, and profitability analysis across all operational tiers.

**Key Financial Highlights:**
- **Year 1 Revenue:** $300K-1.5M USD | PKR 85M-420M
- **Year 3 Revenue:** $4M-12M USD | PKR 1.1B-3.4B
- **Year 5 Revenue:** $25M-80M USD | PKR 7B-22.4B
- **Breakeven:** Month 24-30
- **Target EBITDA Margin:** 25-35%
- **Target Gross Margin:** 50-60%

## 1. Revenue Model Architecture
