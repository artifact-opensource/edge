## Patent Registration Requirements in Pakistan

### Legal Framework
- **Patents Ordinance 2000**: Governing law for patent protection
- **Duration**: 20 years from filing date
- **Renewal**: Annual fees starting 5 years after filing

### Eligibility Criteria
Your invention must be:
1. **Novel**: Not known or used before anywhere in the world
2. **Inventive Step**: Non-obvious to a person skilled in the art
3. **Industrially Applicable**: Capable of being made or used in industry

### Required Documents for Form P-1
- [ ] Completed Form P-1 (Application for patent)
- [ ] Provisional Specification (Form P-3) OR Complete Specification (Form P-3A)
- [ ] Detailed description of the invention (15 sheets)
- [ ] Patent claims defining the scope of protection (3 sheets)
- [ ] Abstract summarizing the invention (1 sheet)
- [ ] Technical drawings/diagrams (5 sheets)
- [ ] Proof of company incorporation (Certificate of Incorporation)
- [ ] Priority documents (if claiming foreign priority)
- [ ] Fee payment receipt

### Patent Application Procedure
1. **File Application**: Submit Form P-1 with all required documents to IPO
2. **Formal Examination**: IPO reviews for completeness (2-3 months)
3. **Publication**: Patent application published after 18 months in Patent Journal
4. **Substantive Examination**: Technical review of novelty and inventive step (12-18 months)
5. **Opposition Period**: 3 months for public opposition after publication
6. **Grant**: Patent certificate issued if no opposition or opposition resolved
7. **Annual Maintenance**: Pay renewal fees annually after 5 years

**Timeline**: 18 months to 2+ years for complete process

### Fees (Approximate - verify with IPO)
- Filing fee: PKR 8,000 - 12,000
- Examination fee: PKR 4,000 - 6,000
- Publication fee: PKR 2,000 - 3,000
- Grant fee: PKR 5,000 - 8,000
- Annual renewal fees: Variable (increases over time)

6. ✓ **"CTHULU"** - Product name
7. ✓ **"GLADIUS"** - Product name

### Legal Framework
- **Trade Marks Ordinance 2001**: Governing law
- **Duration**: 10 years from registration date
- **Renewal**: Renewable every 10 years indefinitely

### Trademark Eligibility
Your trademark must be:
1. **Distinctive**: Not generic or descriptive
2. **Non-Confusing**: Not similar to existing registered marks
3. **Legal**: Not offensive, deceptive, or prohibited

### Required Documents for Form TM-1
- [ ] Completed Form TM-1 (Application to register a trademark)
- [ ] CNIC of applicant OR company registration documents (Certificate of Incorporation)
- [ ] Trademark/logo in JPEG format (high resolution)
- [ ] Business address and contact information
- [ ] Description of business and goods/services to be covered
- [ ] List of classes (Nice Classification) for goods/services
- [ ] Pay Order/Bank Draft for filing fee (typically PKR 3,000 per class)
- [ ] Priority documents (if claiming foreign priority)

### Trademark Registration Procedure
1. **Preliminary Search**: Search IPO online database for conflicting marks at [ipo.gov.pk](https://ipo.gov.pk)
2. **File Application**: Submit Form TM-1 with all documents to IPO
3. **Formal Examination**: IPO reviews for completeness (1-2 months)
4. **Substantive Examination**: Review for distinctiveness and conflicts (3-6 months)
5. **Publication**: Approved marks published in Trademarks Journal
6. **Opposition Period**: 2 months for public opposition
7. **Registration**: Certificate issued if unopposed (or opposition resolved)

**Timeline**: 12-18 months for complete process

## Additional IP Protections to Consider

### 1. Copyright Registration
**What**: Protect original creative works (documentation, marketing materials, website content)
- **Law**: Copyright Ordinance 1962
- **Duration**: Author's lifetime + 50 years
- **Registration**: Optional but recommended for evidence
- **Authority**: Intellectual Property Organization Pakistan

### 2. Industrial Design Registration
**What**: Protect visual design/appearance of products (UI/UX designs)
- **Duration**: 15 years
- **Requirement**: Novel and original design

### 3. Trade Secrets
**What**: Confidential business information (algorithms, source code, business methods)
- **Protection**: Internal confidentiality policies, NDAs, access controls
- **No Registration**: Protected through secrecy

## Important Notes & Best Practices

### 1. Timing is Critical
- **File Early**: Pakistan operates on "first-to-file" system
- **Priority**: Filing date establishes priority over later applicants
- **Public Disclosure**: Do not publicly disclose invention before filing

### 2. Professional Representation
- While not required, hiring an IP attorney/agent is strongly recommended for:
  - Proper claim drafting
  - Navigating examination process
  - Responding to office actions
  - Opposition proceedings

### 3. International Protection
- Pakistan IP rights are territorial (valid only in Pakistan)
- For international protection, consider:
  - **PCT**: Patent Cooperation Treaty for patents
  - **Madrid Protocol**: International trademark system
  - **Paris Convention**: Priority rights in other countries

### 4. Enforcement
- IP rights must be actively enforced by the owner
- Monitor for infringement
- Take legal action when necessary
- Customs recordation to prevent imports of counterfeit goods

### 5. Documentation
- Keep detailed records of:
  - Invention development process
  - Dates of conception and reduction to practice
  - All correspondence with IPO
  - Filing receipts and certificates
