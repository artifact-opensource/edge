## Monitoring & Enforcement

### After Filing:
- [ ] Monitor IPO website for examination reports
- [ ] Respond promptly to any office actions (usually 1-2 months deadline)
- [ ] Monitor Trademarks Journal for publication
- [ ] Watch for oppositions during 2-month window
- [ ] Pay registration fees upon approval

### After Registration:
- [ ] Display ® symbol on all products/marketing materials
- [ ] Monitor market for unauthorized use
- [ ] File complaints with IPO for infringement
- [ ] Consider customs recordation to prevent imports
- [ ] Renew trademarks every 10 years (set reminders)
