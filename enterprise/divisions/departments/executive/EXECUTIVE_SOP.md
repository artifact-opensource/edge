### 1.1 Annual Planning

**Planning Cycle:**
| Month | Activity |
|-------|----------|

| November | Goal setting |

## 4. Decision Making

### 4.1 Decision Framework

**Decision Categories:**
| Type | Timeline | Approver |
|------|----------|----------|
| Operational | Same day | Department head |
| Tactical | 1 week | VP/C-level |
| Strategic | 2-4 weeks | Executive team/Board |

### 4.2 Executive Decisions

**Decision Process:**
1. Issue identification
2. Data gathering
3. Options analysis
4. Recommendation
5. Decision
6. Communication
7. Execution tracking

### 4.3 Escalation

**Escalation Path:**
- Department → VP
- VP → C-level
- C-level → CEO
- CEO → Board
