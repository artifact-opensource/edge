# Chief Executive Officer (CEO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) serves as the highest-ranking executive and primary decision-maker for Artifact Virtual. This role is responsible for the overall strategic direction, operational excellence, and long-term success of the enterprise across AI/ML infrastructure, data center operations, and software product development.

**Reports to:** Board of Directors  
**Direct Reports:** All C-suite executives, VPs of AVRD, AVML, AVRM  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with global remote coordination)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Strategic Leadership & Vision
- Define and communicate the company's long-term vision, mission, and strategic objectives
- Lead the development and execution of multi-year strategic plans across all business units
- Identify and evaluate new market opportunities, product lines, and growth vectors
- Make critical decisions on resource allocation, M&A activities, and strategic partnerships
- Drive innovation initiatives across AI/ML research, infrastructure, and product development
- Establish and maintain the company's competitive positioning in global markets

### 2. Business Performance & Growth
- Own overall P&L responsibility and drive revenue growth targets
- Set and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) across all departments
- Oversee product portfolio strategy and prioritization (18 active projects)
- Lead fundraising efforts, investor relations, and stakeholder communications
- Drive market expansion in Pakistan, US, EU, and emerging markets
- Ensure achievement of quarterly and annual business objectives

### 3. Organizational Development
- Build and lead a high-performing executive team (13 departments)
- Foster a culture of excellence, innovation, and accountability
- Define organizational structure and reporting relationships
- Champion talent development and succession planning
- Drive diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives
- Establish company values and ensure cultural alignment

### 4. Governance & Risk Management
- Ensure 100% GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) adherence
- Lead board meetings and provide comprehensive reporting
- Oversee enterprise risk management framework
- Ensure compliance with SECP regulations, GDPR, PTA, and international standards
- Maintain fiduciary responsibility and ethical business practices
- Protect shareholder interests and maximize enterprise value

### 5. Stakeholder Management
- Serve as primary spokesperson and face of the company
- Build and maintain relationships with key investors, partners, and customers
- Lead customer relationship management for strategic accounts
- Manage government relations and regulatory engagement
- Represent the company at industry events, conferences, and media
- Drive public relations and corporate communications strategy

### 6. Technology & Innovation Leadership
- Champion AI/ML research and development initiatives
- Oversee technology roadmap and product innovation
- Ensure competitive technical capabilities across all platforms
- Drive digital transformation and operational technology adoption
- Sponsor flagship products (HEKTOR, CTHULU, GLADIUS, Artifact ERP)
- Foster partnerships with academic and research institutions

### 7. Operational Excellence
- Establish operational frameworks and performance standards
- Oversee execution of strategic initiatives across all divisions
- Drive operational efficiency and cost optimization
- Ensure world-class customer experience and satisfaction
- Monitor competitive landscape and market trends
- Make real-time adjustments to strategy based on market feedback
