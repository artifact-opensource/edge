# Chief Financial Officer (CFO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) serves as the strategic financial leader and steward of capital for Artifact Virtual. This role is responsible for all financial operations, fundraising strategy, investor relations, financial planning & analysis, and ensuring the company's financial foundation supports aggressive growth in AI/ML infrastructure and enterprise software markets. The CFO must navigate the unique challenges of operating a Pakistan-based technology company with global ambitions, managing multi-currency operations, and building financial systems that scale from startup to enterprise.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**Direct Reports:** VP Finance, Controller, Head of FP&A, Procurement Manager  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with international travel to US/EU)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Financial Strategy & Planning
- Develop and execute comprehensive financial strategy aligned with business objectives
- Lead annual budgeting, quarterly forecasting, and long-range financial planning (5+ years)
- Build sophisticated financial models for scenario planning and strategic decision-making
- Establish and optimize unit economics across all product lines and customer segments
- Define and monitor SaaS metrics (ARR, MRR, CAC, LTV, churn, NRR, gross margin, burn multiple)
- Drive capital efficiency initiatives to maximize runway and minimize dilution
- Develop pricing and packaging strategies across infrastructure, software, and consulting services

### 2. Fundraising & Investor Relations
- Lead all fundraising activities from seed through Series B+ rounds
- Build and maintain relationships with venture capital, private equity, and strategic investors
- Develop compelling financial narratives and investor pitch materials
- Manage cap table, ownership structure, and equity allocation strategies
- Prepare and deliver board presentations on financial performance and strategy
- Coordinate due diligence processes for fundraising and M&A activities
- Maintain ongoing investor communication and reporting cadence
- Navigate Pakistan's regulatory environment for foreign investment (SECP, SBP, BOI)

### 3. Financial Operations & Reporting
- Oversee all accounting operations, ensuring IFRS and Pakistan Companies Act compliance
- Establish robust financial controls, policies, and procedures
- Manage monthly close process and deliver accurate, timely financial statements
- Implement and optimize Artifact ERP for financial management
- Ensure accurate revenue recognition for SaaS, infrastructure, and professional services
- Manage cash flow, treasury operations, and banking relationships
- Oversee payroll, benefits administration, and equity compensation programs
- Maintain audit-ready financial records and coordinate annual audits

### 4. Business Performance Management
- Design and maintain executive dashboards and KPI tracking systems
- Partner with department heads to establish budgets and monitor performance
- Conduct detailed variance analysis and identify corrective actions
- Lead monthly and quarterly business review processes
- Analyze product-level profitability and contribution margins
- Evaluate investment opportunities and provide ROI analysis
- Track competitive benchmarks and financial market comparisons
- Drive data-driven decision-making across the organization

### 5. Risk Management & Compliance
- Establish enterprise risk management framework and controls
- Manage financial risk including currency exposure, credit risk, and market risk
- Ensure compliance with Pakistan tax regulations (FBR) and international tax considerations
- Oversee insurance programs (D&O, E&O, cyber, property, etc.)
- Coordinate with General Counsel on regulatory compliance
- Implement internal audit function and control testing
- Monitor economic and political risks in Pakistan and international markets
- Maintain business continuity planning from financial perspective

### 6. Strategic Business Partnering
- Serve as strategic advisor to CEO and executive team on business decisions
- Evaluate M&A opportunities, strategic partnerships, and divestitures
- Lead negotiation of major customer contracts and partnership agreements
- Provide financial analysis for new market entry and expansion decisions
- Support product strategy with pricing optimization and margin analysis
- Partner with CTO on infrastructure investment and capacity planning
- Collaborate with COO on operational efficiency and scaling initiatives
- Work with CMO on marketing ROI and customer acquisition economics

### 7. Systems & Infrastructure
- Implement world-class finance systems and tools (ERP, FP&A, BI platforms)
- Establish data governance and financial data architecture
- Automate financial reporting and analysis processes
- Build scalable finance operations that support 10x growth
- Implement procurement and vendor management systems
- Establish expense management and approval workflows
- Develop integration between finance and other business systems
- Ensure SOC 2 compliance for financial systems and controls

## Success Metrics

### Year 1 (Foundation)
- Successfully implement robust financial controls and accounting systems (100% IFRS compliance)
- Complete annual audit with clean opinion
- Raise seed or Series A funding ($2M-5M)
- Establish financial planning process with accurate forecasting (±15% accuracy)
- Build finance team foundation (3-5 professionals)
- Implement comprehensive financial dashboard and reporting
- Achieve 100% on-time financial close (within 10 business days)
- Establish board reporting cadence with >90% board satisfaction

### Year 2 (Growth)
- Successfully manage cash to extend runway to 24+ months
- Complete Series A or Series A+ funding round ($5M-10M)
- Maintain gross margin at 65%+ across product portfolio
- Improve CAC payback period to <18 months
- Scale finance team to 8-12 professionals
- Achieve ±10% forecast accuracy on quarterly basis
- Implement automated financial reporting and analytics
- Maintain burn multiple <1.5x

### Year 3 (Scale)
- Lead Series B fundraising round ($15M-25M)
- Achieve path to profitability with clear unit economics
- Maintain NRR >115% for enterprise customers
- Optimize LTV:CAC ratio to >5:1
- Scale finance organization to 15-20 professionals
- Complete SOC 2 Type II audit for financial controls
- Achieve ±5% forecast accuracy on quarterly basis
- Position company for potential IPO or strategic exit

### Long-Term (5+ Years)
- Successfully lead company to IPO or strategic exit at attractive valuation
- Build $100M+ ARR business with strong financial fundamentals
- Maintain >70% gross margin and >25% operating margin
- Develop finance leaders who go on to CFO roles elsewhere
- Establish Artifact Virtual as financial benchmark in AI/ML infrastructure sector
- Create significant shareholder value through capital efficiency and strategic financial management
