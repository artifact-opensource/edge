# Chief Financial Officer (CFO) - Detailed Role Description

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Summary

The Chief Financial Officer is the financial architect and capital steward of Artifact Virtual, responsible for building world-class financial operations that support the company's ambitious growth trajectory in AI/ML infrastructure. This role demands a unique combination of technical accounting excellence, strategic financial planning, fundraising prowess, and operational discipline to navigate the complexities of scaling a Pakistan-based technology company with global reach.

---

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

### Morning Routine (7:00 AM - 10:00 AM)

**Financial Performance Review (45 minutes)**
- Review daily cash position and burn rate against forecast
- Check revenue metrics (ARR, MRR, bookings, collections)
- Monitor key SaaS metrics dashboards (NRR, churn, CAC payback)
- Review overnight financial system alerts and exceptions
- Scan investor and board communication for urgent items
- Check currency exchange rates and financial market conditions

**Treasury & Cash Management (30 minutes)**
- Review cash flow forecast for next 30/60/90 days
- Monitor accounts receivable aging and collection activities
- Oversee accounts payable and upcoming obligations
- Manage wire transfers and international payments
- Review banking activity and reconciliations
- Coordinate with treasury team on daily cash positioning

**Finance Team Sync (30 minutes)**
- Daily standup with Controller, FP&A lead, and key finance managers
- Address immediate blockers on month-end close, reporting, or analysis
- Review progress on key finance initiatives and projects
- Align on day's priorities and critical deliverables
- Discuss any issues requiring CFO attention or escalation

**Strategic Planning Time (45 minutes)**
- Deep work on financial strategy and long-term planning
- Scenario modeling for growth strategies and market opportunities
- Fundraising preparation and investor relationship management
- Financial analysis for strategic initiatives
- Continuous learning on market trends and financial best practices

### Midday Activities (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM)

**Executive Leadership (Variable)**
- Weekly executive team meetings with CEO and C-suite peers
- Strategic business reviews with department heads
- Product and pricing strategy sessions
- M&A evaluation and business development discussions
- Capital allocation and investment decision meetings
- Cross-functional problem-solving and decision-making

**Business Partnership (Ongoing)**
- Partner meetings with CEO on strategic initiatives
- Collaborate with CTO on infrastructure investment decisions
- Work with COO on operational efficiency and scaling
- Support CMO on customer acquisition economics and ROI
- Advise department heads on budgeting and resource allocation
- Provide financial guidance on major contracts and partnerships

**Financial Operations Management (Variable)**
- Review and approve contracts and commitments >$50K
- Month-end close oversight and financial statement review
- Audit management and external auditor coordination
- Financial system optimization and process improvements
- Vendor management and procurement oversight
- Compensation planning and equity administration

### Afternoon Focus (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM)

**Investor Relations & Fundraising (Variable)**
- Investor meetings and pitch presentations
- Fundraising materials preparation (pitch deck, financial model, data room)
- Due diligence coordination for fundraising rounds
- Board presentation preparation and materials review
- Investor update emails and quarterly reporting
- Network building with VCs, growth equity, and advisors

**Financial Planning & Analysis (Variable)**
- Quarterly and annual planning processes
- Detailed variance analysis and forecast updates
- Unit economics modeling and optimization
- Customer cohort analysis and retention metrics
- Product-level profitability analysis
- Competitive financial benchmarking

**Compliance & Risk Management (Variable)**
- GRC framework oversight and compliance monitoring
- Tax planning and optimization strategies
- Insurance review and risk assessment
- Internal control testing and audit preparation
- Regulatory compliance monitoring (SECP, FBR, etc.)
- Policy development and procedure documentation

### Evening Work (6:00 PM - 9:00 PM)

**Communication & Administration**
- Email management and stakeholder communication
- Board member updates and informal touchpoints
- International investor calls (US/EU time zones)
- Financial report reviews and sign-offs
- Strategic reading and market research

**Team Development & Leadership**
- 1-on-1 meetings with direct reports (weekly rotation)
- Performance reviews and career development discussions
- Finance team coaching and mentoring
- Hiring interviews for finance roles
- Professional development and team building

### Weekly Cadence

**Monday: Week Planning & Performance**
- Week kickoff with finance team
- Review prior week actuals vs. forecast
- Prioritize week's critical deliverables
- Executive team meeting with CEO and C-suite
- Cash flow and burn rate analysis

**Tuesday: Business Operations**
- Department head budget reviews
- Operational metrics deep-dives
- Procurement and vendor management
- Systems and process optimization
- Accounts receivable and collections focus

**Wednesday: Strategic Analysis**
- Product-level profitability analysis
- Customer cohort and retention metrics
- Pricing and packaging optimization
- New market opportunity evaluation
- Competitive intelligence review

**Thursday: Planning & Forecasting**
- FP&A team working sessions
- Rolling forecast updates
- Scenario planning and modeling
- Long-range financial plan updates
- Capital allocation reviews

**Friday: Investor & Stakeholder Relations**
- Board materials preparation
- Investor meeting preparation or execution
- Fundraising activities and networking
- External reporting and communications
- Week retrospective and next week planning

**Monthly: Close & Reporting**
- Days 1-5: Month-end close management
- Days 6-10: Financial statement review and board reporting
- Days 11-15: Detailed variance analysis and commentary
- Days 16-20: Planning updates and forecast refresh
- Days 21-31: Strategic projects and continuous improvement

**Quarterly: Strategic Reviews**
- Week 1: Quarterly close and audit coordination
- Week 2: Board meeting preparation and presentation
- Week 3: Quarterly business reviews with departments
- Week 4: Rolling 4-quarter forecast update

## Decision-Making Authority

### Full Authority (No Approval Required)
- Financial operations and day-to-day management
- Budget reallocation within approved annual budget (<10% variance)
- Vendor contracts <$50K annually
- Hiring decisions for finance team (non-executive roles)
- Financial policies and procedures
- Month-end close decisions and accounting treatments
- Banking operations and treasury management
- Expense approvals within policy limits

### Requires CEO Approval
- Annual budget and financial plan
- Capital expenditures >$100K
- Major vendor contracts >$100K annually
- Material changes to financial forecast (>15% variance)
- New banking relationships or credit facilities
- Compensation changes outside standard bands
- Organization structure changes in finance
- Customer contracts with unusual financial terms

### Requires Board Approval
- Fundraising and equity issuance
- Annual operating and capital budgets
- Debt financing arrangements
- Audit firm selection
- Related party transactions
- Executive compensation (CFO and C-suite)
- Major accounting policy changes
- Dividend or distribution policies
- M&A transactions

### Requires Board Notification
- Material changes to financial forecast (>20% variance)
- Significant accounting issues or audit findings
- Regulatory investigations or material compliance issues
- Major customer payment defaults (>$250K)
- Foreign exchange losses >$100K
- Legal judgments or settlements >$100K
- Breach of debt covenants or banking agreements
- Material changes to cap table or ownership

## Success Profile

### Traits of Successful CFOs in This Role

**The Strategic Financial Leader**
- Thinks beyond accounting to strategic financial management
- Bridges finance and business strategy seamlessly
- Proactive in identifying opportunities and risks
- Respected voice in executive team decisions

**The Capital Efficiency Expert**
- Obsessed with unit economics and capital efficiency
- Maximizes value from every dollar invested
- Skilled at extending runway and minimizing dilution
- Disciplined approach to resource allocation

**The Fundraising Champion**
- Natural storyteller who translates numbers into narratives
- Confident and credible in investor meetings
- Builds strong relationships with capital providers
- Successfully closes rounds at attractive terms

**The Systems Thinker**
- Designs scalable financial operations and systems
- Leverages technology to automate and improve
- Thinks in processes, not just transactions
- Builds foundation for future scale

**The Trusted Advisor**
- Business partner, not just financial gatekeeper
- Provides balanced perspective on risks and opportunities
- Earns trust through transparency and integrity
- Sought out for advice on critical decisions

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Strategic Pitfalls:**
- Being too conservative and limiting growth potential
- Focusing on accounting perfection at expense of strategic value
- Failing to push back on unrealistic financial plans
- Missing early warning signs of financial trouble

**Operational Pitfalls:**
- Neglecting to build scalable systems and processes
- Allowing financial close to become too lengthy
- Inadequate cash flow management and forecasting
- Poor communication with non-finance stakeholders

**Fundraising Pitfalls:**
- Starting fundraising too late (running out of cash)
- Over-promising financial projections to investors
- Accepting bad deal terms due to time pressure
- Failing to maintain investor relationships between rounds

**People Pitfalls:**
- Not investing enough in building strong finance team
- Doing too much hands-on work instead of delegating
- Failing to develop future finance leaders
- Creating culture of fear rather than empowerment

## Onboarding & First 90 Days

### First 30 Days: Assess & Understand

**Financial Deep Dive:**
- Review complete financial statements for past 3 years
- Understand revenue model, cost structure, and unit economics
- Analyze cash flow, burn rate, and runway
- Review cap table, equity structure, and investor terms
- Assess accounting systems, controls, and processes
- Identify immediate financial risks or issues

**Stakeholder Meetings:**
- Meet with CEO for alignment on priorities and expectations
- Interview all C-suite executives and understand their priorities
- Meet with board members and investors individually
- Connect with external auditors and advisors
- Interview all finance team members
- Speak with 5-10 key customers about contracts and payment terms

**Assessment Deliverables:**
- Financial health assessment and risk identification
- Finance team capabilities and gaps analysis
- Systems and process improvement opportunities
- 90-day priority plan with quick wins

### Days 31-60: Plan & Stabilize

**Quick Wins Execution:**
- Fix any immediate financial reporting or closing issues
- Improve cash visibility and forecasting accuracy
- Establish key financial dashboards and metrics
- Address any urgent compliance or audit issues
- Optimize banking relationships and payment processes

**Strategic Planning:**
- Develop comprehensive financial plan and budget
- Build detailed financial model and fundraising scenarios
- Establish KPIs and measurement framework
- Design executive financial dashboard
- Create board reporting template and cadence

**Team Building:**
- Assess team capabilities and identify hiring needs
- Establish regular team meetings and communication cadence
- Begin recruiting for critical open positions
- Provide clarity on roles, responsibilities, and expectations

### Days 61-90: Execute & Accelerate

**Strategic Initiatives Launch:**
- Present financial strategy to CEO and board
- Begin fundraising preparation if needed
- Implement priority system and process improvements
- Launch finance team development initiatives
- Establish regular business partnership rhythm with executives

**Momentum Building:**
- Deliver first board presentation as CFO
- Complete first monthly close under new leadership
- Demonstrate early wins and improvements
- Build credibility with CEO, board, and investors
- Set tone for finance culture and excellence

## Compensation & Benefits

**Note:** Specific compensation details are maintained separately and reviewed by the Board Compensation Committee. General framework:

- **Base Salary:** PKR 10-15M annually (approximately $35K-55K USD), competitive for Pakistan market with international company
- **Equity:** 2-4% ownership stake (typically with 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff)
- **Performance Bonus:** 20-40% of base salary tied to KPIs and company performance
- **Benefits:** Health insurance (premium coverage), retirement benefits, life insurance
- **Perquisites:** Housing allowance, vehicle allowance, professional development budget ($10K+/year), CFO conference attendance

## Evaluation & Review Process

### Annual Performance Review
- Conducted by CEO with Board input (typically Q4)
- Based on achievement of financial KPIs and strategic objectives
- 360-degree feedback from board, CEO, C-suite peers, and finance team
- Self-assessment on leadership and development
- Compensation review and equity grant consideration
- Goal setting for upcoming year aligned with company strategy

### Quarterly Check-ins
- Board meetings include CFO performance and financial review
- Progress against quarterly OKRs and financial targets
- Fundraising updates and capital strategy discussions
- Course correction on financial plan as needed
- Support and resource needs assessment

### Continuous Feedback
- Weekly 1-on-1s with CEO
- Regular feedback from board members and investors
- Finance team upward feedback
- Cross-functional partner feedback
- Self-reflection and continuous improvement

### Board Financial Reporting
- Monthly financial package (within 10 days of month-end)
- Quarterly board presentation (comprehensive review)
- Annual budget and strategic plan presentation
- Special presentations for fundraising, M&A, or strategic initiatives
