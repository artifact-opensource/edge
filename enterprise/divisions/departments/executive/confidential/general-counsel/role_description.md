# General Counsel (Chief Legal Officer) - Detailed Role Description

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Summary

The General Counsel (Chief Legal Officer) is the chief legal architect and strategic counselor for Artifact Virtual, responsible for building and maintaining the legal infrastructure that enables the company's ambitious growth trajectory across AI/ML technology, data center operations, and international markets. This role demands exceptional expertise in Pakistan corporate law combined with international commercial sophistication, providing strategic legal leadership for intellectual property protection, regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and complex commercial transactions in a high-growth technology environment.

---

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

### Morning Routine (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM)

**Daily Legal Review (45 minutes)**
- Review overnight legal requests and urgent matters from international time zones
- Check regulatory updates from SECP, PTA, FBR, and international regulators
- Scan legal news and developments in technology law, IP, privacy, and corporate law
- Review critical contract negotiations and pending legal matters
- Prioritize top 3-5 legal issues for immediate attention

**Executive Legal Briefing (30 minutes)**
- Daily or weekly sync with CEO on legal and regulatory matters
- Provide legal updates on critical transactions and compliance issues
- Discuss upcoming board matters and governance issues
- Align on legal strategy for major business initiatives
- Address immediate legal questions from executive team

**Compliance & Risk Assessment (30 minutes)**
- Review compliance dashboard and regulatory tracking systems
- Monitor pending regulatory filings and submission deadlines
- Assess emerging legal risks across business operations
- Review legal holds and litigation management status
- Coordinate with compliance officer on priority matters

**Contract Review & Negotiation (60 minutes)**
- Review and approve priority customer contracts and amendments
- Negotiate key terms with partners, vendors, and strategic counterparties
- Provide markup and comments on complex commercial agreements
- Escalate significant legal or business issues to appropriate stakeholders
- Manage contract approval workflow and sign-offs

### Midday Activities (11:00 AM - 2:00 PM)

**Strategic Legal Counsel (Variable)**
- Advise CEO and executives on strategic legal matters
- Board meeting preparation and governance matters
- Investor discussions and fundraising legal support
- M&A transaction support and due diligence oversight

**Regulatory & Compliance Activities (Variable)**

- Government relations and regulatory agency meetings

### Afternoon Focus (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM)

- Review and quality control of work product from team members

**Department Support & Cross-Functional Work (Variable)**

**Transaction & Deal Work (Variable)**

## Decision-Making Authority

### Full Authority (No Approval Required)

- Legal advice and counsel to management on day-to-day matters
- Contract review and approval up to $100K annual value
- Routine SECP filings and corporate secretary matters
- Standard employment agreements and HR documentation
- Engagement of external counsel for routine matters (within budget)
- Legal department operations and process management
- Non-material trademark and IP registrations
- Standard vendor contract approvals (using approved templates)
- Legal policy implementation and updates (non-material)
- Routine regulatory correspondence and compliance activities

### Requires CEO Approval

- Material contracts over $100K annual value
- Litigation strategy and settlement over $50K
- Major corporate transactions (fundraising, M&A, partnerships)
- Engagement of external counsel for major matters (>$25K)
- Material changes to legal policies or governance frameworks
- Establishment of international legal entities or structures
- Material IP decisions (patent filing strategy, major licensing deals)
- Regulatory matters with significant business impact
- Hiring of legal team members
- Legal budget changes >10% of approved budget

### Requires Board Approval

- Fundraising legal documentation (term sheets, shareholder agreements)
- M&A transactions and material strategic transactions
- Material litigation settlements over $250K
- Changes to corporate governance documents (bylaws, etc.)
- Related party transactions requiring board approval
- Equity compensation plans and material amendments
- Material changes to capital structure
- Matters involving director liability or conflicts of interest
- Major regulatory settlements or consent orders
- Establishment of significant legal contingencies

### Requires Board Notification

- Material litigation filed against the company
- Significant regulatory investigations or enforcement actions
- Material contract disputes or commercial litigation
- IP infringement claims (as plaintiff or defendant)
- Data breaches or privacy incidents requiring notification
- Material changes in legal or regulatory landscape
- Significant legal spend variances from budget
- Key legal team hires or departures
- Material legal risk developments
- Corporate governance best practice updates

## Success Profile

### Traits of Successful General Counsel in This Role

**The Business-Focused Legal Strategist**
- Deeply understands business objectives and translates into legal strategy
- Balances legal risk with commercial opportunity and growth
- Provides solutions, not just identification of problems
- Trusted advisor to CEO and executive team on business matters
- Anticipates legal needs before they become issues

**The Pakistan Legal Expert with International Sophistication**
- Mastery of Pakistan corporate law and regulatory landscape
- Comfortable navigating SECP, PTA, FBR, and government agencies
- Sophisticated understanding of international commercial law
- Experience with US and EU legal systems and cross-border transactions
- Cultural fluency across Pakistani, American, and European contexts

**The Technology & IP Savvy Lawyer**
- Deep understanding of AI/ML legal issues and emerging technology regulation
- Strong IP strategy capability for protecting and monetizing innovations
- Practical knowledge of data protection and privacy law
- Understanding of blockchain, smart contracts, and digital assets
- Ability to engage meaningfully with technical teams

**The Transaction Leader**
- Proven track record leading complex corporate transactions
- Exceptional negotiation skills with ability to close deals
- Experience with venture capital fundraising and startup legal matters
- Capability to manage multiple transactions simultaneously
- Strong business judgment on deal structure and risk allocation

**The Pragmatic Risk Manager**
- Excellent judgment on risk calibration and risk/reward tradeoffs
- Provides practical advice that enables business while managing risk
- Knows when to hold firm and when to find creative solutions
- Builds trust through consistent, reliable counsel
- Transparent about risks without being unnecessarily conservative

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Legal Pitfalls:**
- Over-lawyering transactions and slowing business velocity unnecessarily
- Under-managing critical legal risks leading to compliance failures
- Failing to anticipate legal needs proactively (purely reactive)
- Poor communication of legal risks to non-legal audiences
- Not building sufficient external counsel relationships for specialized needs

**Business Partnership Pitfalls:**
- Failing to understand business strategy and priorities
- Saying "no" without providing alternative paths forward
- Not building trust and credibility with CEO and executive team
- Isolating legal function from business operations
- Providing legally perfect advice that is commercially impractical

**Regulatory Pitfalls:**
- Missing regulatory filing deadlines or compliance requirements
- Failing to maintain productive relationships with regulators
- Not staying ahead of regulatory changes in fast-moving areas (AI, data protection)
- Under-investing in compliance programs until crisis emerges
- Not building political and policy awareness in regulatory matters

**Team & Operations Pitfalls:**
- Not building legal team fast enough to support business growth
- Poor management of external counsel leading to cost overruns
- Failing to implement efficient legal operations and processes
- Not leveraging technology for contract and compliance management
- Neglecting professional development for self and team

**International Expansion Pitfalls:**
- Not establishing legal infrastructure early enough for international operations
- Under-estimating complexity of multi-jurisdictional compliance
- Failing to engage appropriate local counsel in target markets
- Not planning for data localization and cross-border data transfer requirements
- Under-managing IP protection in international expansion

## Onboarding & First 90 Days

### First 30 Days: Assess & Understand

**Legal & Compliance Assessment**
- Conduct comprehensive legal and compliance audit
- Review corporate records, SECP filings, and governance documentation
- Assess existing contracts and commercial arrangements
- Review IP portfolio and protection measures
- Evaluate data protection and privacy compliance status
- Identify immediate legal risks and compliance gaps

**Stakeholder Meetings**
- Deep dive sessions with CEO on legal priorities and strategy
- Meet with all executive team members to understand legal needs
- Interview board members on governance expectations
- Engage with external counsel and advisors
- Meet with regulatory contacts (SECP, PTA, etc.)
- Customer and partner discussions on contract issues

**Business Understanding**
- Deep dive into business model, products, and go-to-market strategy
- Understand technology stack and IP portfolio (HEKTOR, GLADIUS, etc.)
- Review financial model, fundraising history, and cap table
- Assess competitive landscape and market position
- Understand growth plans and international expansion strategy

**Initial Priorities Identification**
- Identify top 5-10 legal priorities for first 90 days
- Assess critical compliance gaps requiring immediate attention
- Evaluate urgent contract or transaction needs
- Determine legal team and external counsel requirements
- Develop initial risk assessment and mitigation plan

### Days 31-60: Build & Implement

**Compliance & Governance Framework**
- Address any critical compliance gaps identified
- Implement or enhance corporate governance practices
- Develop or update key legal policies and procedures
- Establish compliance calendar and tracking system
- Begin GDPR compliance program implementation if not complete
- Ensure SECP compliance is current and systems in place

**Legal Operations Setup**
- Establish legal request intake and management process
- Implement or optimize contract management system
- Develop contract templates and playbooks for efficiency
- Set up matter management and outside counsel guidelines
- Create legal dashboard and metrics for tracking performance
- Establish regular communication cadence with stakeholders

**Strategic Legal Planning**
- Develop 12-month legal strategic plan aligned with business objectives
- Present legal priorities and strategy to CEO and board
- Identify legal team hiring needs and recruitment plan
- Assess and optimize external counsel panel
- Plan for major transactions (fundraising, partnerships, etc.)
- Develop IP strategy and filing roadmap

**Quick Wins**
- Close 1-2 pending transactions or contracts
- Resolve outstanding legal issues causing business friction
- Implement efficiency improvements in legal processes
- Build credibility through responsive, solutions-oriented support
- Establish reputation as business partner, not bottleneck

### Days 61-90: Execute & Scale

**Transaction Support**
- Lead priority transactions (fundraising, major contracts, partnerships)
- Support business development with efficient legal review
- Negotiate and close key customer and partner agreements
- Advance M&A or strategic transactions if in pipeline

**Team Building**
- Make key legal team hires if critical gaps exist
- Onboard and train legal team members
- Establish team operating rhythms and processes
- Build external counsel relationships and engagement models

**Board & Executive Partnership**
- Prepare for and present at first board meeting as General Counsel

## Evaluation & Review Process

### Annual Performance Review

- Conducted by CEO with input from Board of Directors (typically Q4)
- Based on achievement of legal KPIs and strategic objectives
- 360-degree feedback from executive team, board members, and key stakeholders
- Self-assessment and reflection on legal function performance
- Forward-looking goal setting for upcoming year
- Compensation review and adjustment

### Quarterly Check-ins

- Regular 1-on-1 meetings with CEO (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Quarterly legal function review with CEO and/or board
- Review of progress against quarterly OKRs and priorities
- Assessment of legal spend vs. budget
- Discussion of emerging legal risks and regulatory changes
- Course correction and resource needs assessment

### Board Reporting

- Legal section of board materials for each board meeting
- Annual legal and compliance report to board
- Periodic deep dives on specific legal topics (IP strategy, regulatory landscape, etc.)
- Board committee reporting (audit committee on compliance and risk)
- Special reporting on material legal matters as they arise

### Continuous Feedback

- Regular feedback from CEO on legal counsel quality
- Input from executive team on legal support effectiveness
- Business unit feedback on contract and transaction support
- External counsel feedback on collaboration and management
- Regulatory relationship health assessment
- Self-reflection and continuous improvement
