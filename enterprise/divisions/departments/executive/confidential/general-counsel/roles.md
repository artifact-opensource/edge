# General Counsel (Chief Legal Officer) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The General Counsel (Chief Legal Officer) serves as the chief legal advisor and strategic counselor to Artifact Virtual, responsible for all legal affairs, regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and risk management for the company's operations across Pakistan, United States, European Union, and emerging markets. This role combines deep expertise in Pakistan corporate law with international commercial acumen, providing sophisticated legal support for AI/ML technology protection, data center operations, international expansion, and venture capital transactions.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Direct Reports:** Legal team, Compliance Officer, Corporate Secretary  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with international coordination)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Corporate Governance & Company Law

- Ensure full compliance with Companies Act 2017 and Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) regulations
- Maintain company registration, corporate records, and statutory registers per SECP requirements
- Oversee all filings with SECP, including annual returns, financial statements, and director changes
- Advise Board of Directors on governance best practices and fiduciary duties
- Prepare and maintain board resolutions, minutes, and corporate documentation
- Manage corporate secretary functions including shareholder meetings and company seal
- Ensure compliance with SMC-Private Limited corporate structure requirements
- Advise on director liability, conflicts of interest, and corporate decision-making
- Oversee related party transaction approvals and disclosure requirements
- Maintain compliance with Companies (General Provisions and Forms) Regulations 2018

### 2. Intellectual Property Strategy & Protection

- Develop and execute comprehensive IP strategy for AI/ML technologies (HEKTOR, GLADIUS, CTHULU)
- Manage patent portfolio including filing, prosecution, and maintenance across multiple jurisdictions
- Protect trade secrets and proprietary algorithms through robust confidentiality frameworks
- Register and maintain trademarks for company and product brands (Pakistan, US, EU)
- Oversee copyright protection for software code, documentation, and creative works
- Negotiate and draft technology licensing agreements (inbound and outbound)
- Advise on open source licensing compliance and strategy (MIT, Apache, GPL)
- Manage IP due diligence for fundraising and M&A activities
- Develop employee invention assignment and IP ownership policies
- Monitor and enforce IP rights against infringement
- Coordinate with international IP counsel for multi-jurisdictional protection

### 3. Data Protection & Privacy Compliance

- Lead GDPR compliance program for EU operations and data processing
- Ensure compliance with Pakistan's data protection frameworks and emerging regulations
- Implement privacy policies and data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)
- Oversee cross-border data transfer mechanisms (Standard Contractual Clauses, Adequacy Decisions)
- Manage data breach response procedures and regulatory notification requirements
- Advise on US privacy laws (CCPA, CPRA, state-specific regulations)
- Develop data retention, deletion, and subject access request procedures
- Negotiate data processing agreements (DPAs) with customers and vendors
- Train employees on data protection requirements and best practices
- Serve as or appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO) for GDPR compliance
- Monitor evolving global privacy regulations and ensure proactive compliance

### 4. Commercial Contracts & Business Transactions

- Draft and negotiate enterprise customer agreements for data center and cloud services
- Structure and negotiate strategic partnership agreements and joint ventures
- Oversee vendor and supplier contracts for data center operations and technology procurement
- Negotiate software licensing agreements (SaaS, perpetual, hybrid models)
- Draft master services agreements (MSAs) and statements of work (SOWs)
- Advise on contract risk allocation, liability caps, and indemnification provisions
- Review and negotiate reseller, distributor, and channel partner agreements
- Manage contract lifecycle including amendments, renewals, and terminations
- Develop standardized contract templates to improve efficiency
- Maintain contract management system and repository
- Train business teams on contract negotiation and key terms

### 5. Regulatory Compliance & Government Relations

- Ensure compliance with Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) regulations
- Manage Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) tax compliance and legal issues
- Navigate State Bank of Pakistan foreign exchange regulations for international transactions
- Obtain and maintain necessary licenses and permits for data center operations
- Manage compliance with cybersecurity regulations and directives
- Advise on power sector regulations and utility contracts
- Handle regulatory investigations and government inquiries
- Build relationships with regulatory agencies and government officials
- Monitor regulatory changes and advise on compliance requirements
- Manage legal aspects of government contracts and public sector business

### 6. International Expansion & Market Entry

- Lead legal strategy for US market entry and operations
- Oversee EU market expansion and legal entity establishment
- Advise on foreign subsidiary formation and corporate structure optimization
- Navigate international tax considerations and treaty benefits
- Ensure compliance with export controls and sanctions (OFAC, EU sanctions)
- Manage international employment law compliance
- Coordinate with international counsel in target markets
- Advise on cross-border data flows and digital services regulations
- Structure international partnerships and distribution arrangements
- Support visa and immigration matters for international expansion

### 7. Employment Law & HR Legal Support

- Ensure compliance with Pakistan labor laws and Industrial Relations Ordinance
- Draft employment agreements, consulting agreements, and independent contractor agreements
- Develop employee handbooks, policies, and workplace procedures
- Advise on terminations, layoffs, and workforce restructuring
- Manage employment disputes and labor law compliance issues
- Oversee equity compensation plans and employee stock option programs (ESOPs)
- Ensure workplace safety compliance and Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) requirements
- Advise on anti-discrimination, harassment, and workplace investigation procedures
- Support international hiring and employment law compliance (US, EU)
- Review and approve non-compete, non-solicitation, and confidentiality agreements

### 8. Fundraising & Corporate Finance Legal

- Lead legal aspects of venture capital fundraising (seed, Series A, Series B+)
- Draft and negotiate term sheets with investors
- Prepare shareholder agreements and investor rights agreements
- Manage cap table and equity ownership documentation
- Oversee due diligence processes for fundraising rounds
- Negotiate board representation and protective provisions
- Advise on company valuation and equity allocation strategies
- Ensure compliance with securities regulations for equity issuance
- Manage convertible notes, SAFEs, and alternative financing instruments
- Coordinate with corporate finance advisors and investment banks
- Prepare disclosure schedules and legal documentation for closings

### 9. Mergers, Acquisitions & Strategic Transactions

- Lead M&A legal strategy and transaction execution
- Conduct legal due diligence on acquisition targets
- Draft letters of intent (LOIs) and memoranda of understanding (MOUs)
- Negotiate purchase agreements (stock purchase, asset purchase)
- Structure tax-efficient transaction frameworks
- Manage post-closing integration and legal matters
- Advise on divestitures and spin-offs
- Evaluate strategic investment opportunities
- Negotiate earn-outs, escrows, and contingent consideration
- Coordinate with external M&A counsel and advisors

### 10. Technology & Blockchain Legal

- Advise on smart contract legal issues and enforceability
- Navigate blockchain regulatory frameworks (Pakistan, international)
- Structure token offerings and digital asset transactions
- Ensure compliance with AML/KYC requirements for blockchain products
- Draft terms of service and user agreements for technology platforms
- Advise on API terms, developer agreements, and platform policies
- Manage legal issues related to AI/ML model deployment and usage
- Address algorithmic bias, fairness, and ethical AI considerations
- Navigate emerging technology regulations (AI Act in EU)
- Protect company from liability in AI-generated content and decisions

### 11. Litigation & Dispute Resolution

- Manage litigation strategy and outside litigation counsel
- Oversee dispute resolution through negotiation, mediation, and arbitration
- Handle pre-litigation demand letters and settlement negotiations
- Manage intellectual property disputes and infringement claims
- Oversee employment-related litigation and labor disputes
- Coordinate responses to customer claims and contract disputes
- Manage regulatory investigations and enforcement actions
- Develop litigation budget and cost management strategies
- Maintain litigation holds and evidence preservation procedures
- Advise executive team on litigation risks and exposure

### 12. Risk Management & Legal Operations

- Develop enterprise-wide legal risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Implement legal compliance programs across all departments
- Manage insurance policies including D&O, E&O, cyber liability, and general liability
- Oversee business continuity planning from legal perspective
- Develop crisis management legal protocols
- Maintain legal risk register and compliance tracking systems
- Implement legal hold and document retention policies
- Manage legal department budget and external legal spend
- Develop metrics and KPIs for legal department performance
- Leverage legal technology and automation tools
