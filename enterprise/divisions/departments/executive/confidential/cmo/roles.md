# Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) serves as the senior executive responsible for all marketing, communications, and brand strategy at Artifact Virtual. This role is critical to establishing market presence in emerging and developed markets, driving demand generation for AI/ML infrastructure products, building developer community engagement, and positioning Artifact Virtual as a technical leader against hyperscaler competitors.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**Direct Reports:** VP Marketing, Director of Communications, Head of Developer Relations, Content Marketing Manager, Growth Marketing Manager, Product Marketing Managers  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with significant international coordination across US/EU markets)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Strategic Marketing Leadership & Brand Vision
- Define and execute comprehensive marketing strategy across Pakistan, US, and EU markets
- Establish Artifact Virtual as a recognized technical leader in AI/ML infrastructure and developer tools
- Build and maintain brand positioning that differentiates from hyperscalers (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Drive thought leadership strategy and executive visibility in AI/ML and infrastructure communities
- Lead competitive positioning and messaging against established cloud providers
- Develop multi-year brand building roadmap for emerging (Pakistan) and developed (US/EU) markets

### 2. Product Marketing & Go-to-Market Strategy
- Own product marketing strategy for flagship products (HEKTOR, GLADIUS, CTHULU, Artifact ERP)
- Lead go-to-market planning and execution for new product launches
- Develop positioning, messaging, and value propositions for technical and developer audiences
- Create buyer personas and ideal customer profiles across enterprise, startup, and research segments
- Drive competitive analysis and market intelligence for product strategy
- Partner with product teams to influence roadmap based on market feedback and positioning opportunities
- Establish product marketing frameworks that scale across 18 active projects

### 3. Demand Generation & Growth Marketing
- Build and execute B2B demand generation engine targeting 10-25 customers Year 1, scaling to 250-400 by Year 5
- Develop multi-channel marketing strategy (content, digital, events, partnerships, community)
- Own marketing pipeline generation targets and contribution to revenue goals
- Implement marketing automation, lead scoring, and nurture programs
- Drive conversion optimization across marketing funnel (awareness → consideration → decision)
- Establish marketing attribution models and ROI measurement frameworks
- Optimize customer acquisition costs (CAC) and improve marketing efficiency metrics

### 4. Developer Relations & Community Building
- Build and scale developer relations program to engage technical audiences
- Establish Artifact Virtual presence in open-source communities and developer ecosystems
- Drive developer advocacy through content, documentation, tutorials, and sample projects
- Create and nurture developer community programs (forums, Discord, GitHub)
- Lead technical content strategy (blog posts, whitepapers, technical documentation)
- Establish developer metrics (community size, engagement, contributions, adoption)
- Partner with engineering teams on open-source strategy and community contributions

### 5. Content Marketing & Thought Leadership
- Own content strategy and editorial calendar across all channels
- Drive technical content creation (whitepapers, case studies, research reports, technical blogs)
- Establish Artifact Virtual as thought leader in AI/ML infrastructure, vector databases, and emerging technologies
- Build executive thought leadership programs (speaking, writing, media)
- Create customer success stories, case studies, and ROI documentation
- Develop educational content for technical decision-makers and practitioners
- Establish content distribution strategy and amplification programs

### 6. Digital Marketing & Performance Optimization
- Lead digital marketing strategy including SEO, SEM, paid advertising, and retargeting
- Build and optimize company website for conversion and technical audience engagement
- Manage marketing technology stack and martech integration
- Drive SEO strategy to establish visibility for key technical and product keywords
- Optimize paid acquisition channels (Google Ads, LinkedIn, technical publications)
- Implement website analytics, conversion tracking, and A/B testing programs
- Establish digital marketing KPIs and performance dashboards

### 7. Event Marketing & Field Programs
- Plan and execute event marketing strategy (conferences, meetups, webinars, workshops)
- Establish Artifact Virtual presence at major industry events (KubeCon, AWS re:Invent, AI conferences)
- Build field marketing programs for Pakistan, US, and EU markets
- Create virtual event strategy (webinars, online workshops, virtual conferences)
- Develop event metrics and ROI frameworks
- Partner with sales on account-based marketing and customer events
- Build local market presence through regional events and meetups

### 8. Sales Enablement & Marketing-Sales Alignment
- Create and maintain sales enablement materials (pitch decks, battle cards, demo scripts)
- Develop customer-facing collateral (one-pagers, solution briefs, ROI calculators)
- Establish service level agreements (SLAs) between marketing and sales teams
- Implement lead handoff processes and qualification criteria
- Create competitive intelligence resources for sales team
- Provide sales training on messaging, positioning, and product differentiation
- Build customer reference programs and analyst relations support for sales cycles

### 9. Public Relations & Media Strategy
- Build and execute PR strategy for technology company targeting global tech media
- Establish media relationships with key technology publications and analysts
- Manage press releases, media briefings, and executive interviews
- Drive analyst relations strategy (Gartner, Forrester, etc.)
- Handle crisis communications and reputation management
- Create PR measurement framework and media impact tracking
- Build media training programs for executives and spokespersons

### 10. Marketing Analytics & Performance Management
- Establish marketing KPIs, metrics, and reporting frameworks
- Implement marketing attribution modeling (multi-touch, first-touch, last-touch)
- Build marketing dashboards and executive reporting
- Drive data-driven decision making across marketing organization
- Analyze campaign performance and optimize based on insights
- Establish ROI frameworks for marketing investments
- Create forecasting models for pipeline and revenue contribution

### 11. Customer Marketing & Advocacy Programs
- Develop customer marketing programs to drive expansion and retention
- Build customer advocacy and reference programs
- Create customer advisory boards and community programs
- Drive case study development and customer storytelling
- Establish customer success content and educational programs
- Build customer feedback loops into product marketing
- Create customer events and engagement programs

### 12. Multi-Market Strategy & Localization
- Develop market-specific strategies for Pakistan, US, and EU markets
- Create localized messaging and positioning for different cultural contexts
- Build understanding of regional competitive dynamics and customer preferences
- Establish marketing partnerships in key geographic markets
- Drive market entry strategies for new regions
- Balance global brand consistency with local market relevance
- Build market intelligence capabilities for each geographic segment

### 13. Marketing Team Building & Leadership
- Recruit, develop, and retain world-class marketing talent in Pakistan market
- Build marketing organization from ground up to support company growth
- Establish marketing team structure, roles, and career paths
- Create high-performance marketing culture aligned with company values
- Develop marketing talent through training, mentoring, and growth opportunities
- Manage marketing budget and resource allocation
- Build remote/distributed marketing team capabilities for international markets
