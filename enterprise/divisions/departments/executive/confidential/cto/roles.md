# Chief Technology Officer (CTO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Technology Officer (CTO) serves as the technical backbone and architectural visionary for Artifact Virtual, responsible for all technology infrastructure, platform engineering, AI/ML operations, data center management, and technical strategy execution. This role demands world-class expertise in distributed systems, cloud architecture, DevOps/MLOps, and physical infrastructure operations while driving technical innovation across all 18 active projects.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**Direct Reports:** VP Engineering, VP Infrastructure, Director of DevOps, Director of Data Center Operations, Director of Platform Engineering, Technical Leads  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with global remote coordination)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Technology Strategy & Architecture
- Define and execute comprehensive technology strategy aligned with business objectives
- Design and evolve enterprise-wide technical architecture for AI/ML infrastructure and products
- Lead technology roadmap development for all major platforms (HEKTOR, CTHULU, GLADIUS, Artifact ERP)
- Make critical build-vs-buy decisions and technology stack selections
- Drive technical innovation and R&D initiatives across the organization
- Establish architectural principles and technical standards for all engineering teams
- Evaluate emerging technologies and assess their strategic fit (quantum computing, blockchain, AI advances)

### 2. Infrastructure & Platform Operations
- Oversee physical data center operations in Islamabad with 99.99% uptime target
- Manage cloud infrastructure strategy across multi-cloud environments (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Lead hybrid cloud architecture design and implementation
- Drive platform engineering initiatives for scalable, self-service infrastructure
- Ensure infrastructure reliability, performance, and cost optimization
- Oversee Kubernetes orchestration, containerization strategy, and microservices architecture
- Manage infrastructure capacity planning and scaling strategies from startup to enterprise

### 3. DevOps, MLOps & Engineering Excellence
- Establish and evolve DevOps culture, practices, and tooling across all teams
- Build MLOps platforms for AI/ML model training, deployment, and monitoring
- Drive CI/CD pipeline optimization and deployment automation
- Implement infrastructure-as-code (IaC) practices and GitOps workflows
- Establish engineering productivity metrics and continuous improvement programs
- Lead technical debt management and code quality initiatives
- Foster engineering excellence through code reviews, testing standards, and documentation

### 4. Security Architecture & Infrastructure Security
- Design defense-in-depth security architecture for all systems and networks
- Oversee infrastructure security, network security, and perimeter defense
- Implement zero-trust architecture and identity management systems
- Lead security hardening of Kubernetes clusters, containers, and cloud environments
- Collaborate with CISO on security strategy and compliance requirements
- Manage security incident response from infrastructure perspective
- Drive encryption strategy including Shield256 implementation

### 5. Technical Leadership & Team Building
- Build and lead world-class engineering, DevOps, and infrastructure teams
- Recruit senior technical talent in competitive Pakistan and global markets
- Mentor and develop technical leaders (senior engineers, architects, directors)
- Foster technical culture of innovation, ownership, and continuous learning
- Drive technical decision-making across product and platform teams
- Establish career development frameworks for technical roles
- Create high-performance engineering culture with strong retention

### 6. Data Center & Physical Infrastructure
- Oversee day-to-day data center operations and facility management
- Ensure redundant power systems, cooling, and environmental controls
- Manage colocation services, dedicated server deployments, and managed hosting
- Drive 99.99% uptime SLA achievement and incident management
- Optimize data center efficiency (PUE, cooling efficiency, power management)
- Plan and execute data center expansion and capacity increases
- Manage vendor relationships for hardware, networking, and infrastructure services

### 7. Product Technology Leadership
- Provide technical leadership for flagship products (HEKTOR, GLADIUS, CTHULU, Artifact ERP)
- Guide technical architecture for HEKTOR spectral vector database
- Oversee CTHULU trading platform infrastructure and performance optimization
- Lead GLADIUS AI technology development and deployment
- Drive Artifact ERP technical platform evolution
- Ensure blockchain/EVM infrastructure for ARC and Outcome projects
- Balance innovation with reliability and maintainability

### 8. Open Source Strategy & Community
- Define open-source strategy and community engagement approach
- Lead technical contributions to major open-source projects
- Manage open-source product releases and community development
- Build developer relations and technical community programs
- Drive inner-source practices within the organization
- Foster technical brand building through open-source leadership
- Manage relationships with open-source foundations and communities
