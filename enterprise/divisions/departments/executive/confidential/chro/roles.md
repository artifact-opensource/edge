# Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) serves as the strategic leader of all people operations for Artifact Virtual, responsible for building and scaling a world-class engineering organization from 5-10 employees to 100+ employees across Pakistan and international markets. This role demands expertise in technical recruiting, compensation design, culture scaling, and global workforce management within the AI/ML infrastructure and enterprise software sectors.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**Direct Reports:** VP Talent Acquisition, HR Operations Manager, Learning & Development Manager  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with international remote team coordination)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Talent Acquisition & Technical Recruiting
- Lead recruiting strategy for highly competitive AI/ML, data science, and DevOps engineering talent
- Build talent pipelines for Pakistan market (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi) leveraging limited local tech talent pool
- Develop international remote hiring strategies for US and EU markets (competitive compensation, remote work policies)
- Establish relationships with top CS programs (NUST, FAST, LUMS, Punjab University) and international universities
- Drive diversity recruiting initiatives targeting 40%+ diverse leadership by Year 3
- Oversee recruitment of 10-15 employees Year 1, 30+ Year 2, 50+ Year 3 to reach 100+ total headcount
- Own engineering hiring metrics: time-to-hire <45 days, offer acceptance rate >75%, quality-of-hire scores >4/5
- Partner with CEO on executive recruiting (C-suite, VPs, senior directors)
- Build employer brand in Pakistan tech community and internationally

### 2. Compensation & Benefits Strategy
- Design competitive compensation structures for Pakistan market (benchmarking against local tech companies and multinationals)
- Create international compensation frameworks for US/EU remote employees (market-rate salaries, location adjustments)
- Implement equity compensation program: stock options, RSUs, employee stock ownership
- Design vesting schedules aligned with retention targets (4-year vests with 1-year cliffs)
- Develop performance-based bonus programs tied to OKRs and company milestones
- Structure benefits packages: health insurance, retirement plans (EOBI, provident fund), professional development budgets
- Manage compensation across multiple currencies (PKR, USD, EUR) and cost-of-living adjustments
- Ensure compensation competitiveness: target 75th percentile for Pakistan, 50th-75th percentile for international markets
- Own total compensation budget and cost-per-employee optimization

### 3. Organizational Design & Workforce Planning
- Scale organizational structure from 5-10 employees → 20 (Year 1) → 50 (Year 2) → 100+ (Year 3)
- Design department structures for 13 departments (Executive, AVRD, AVML, AVRM, Operations, Finance, HR, Legal, Marketing, IT, Security, plus divisions)
- Develop reporting relationships and span-of-control guidelines (target 5-7 direct reports per manager)
- Plan headcount growth aligned with revenue targets: $2M ARR (20 employees), $5M ARR (50 employees), $20M ARR (100+ employees)
- Create role definitions and career ladders for engineering (IC track: Engineer I-V, Staff, Principal, Distinguished; Management track: EM, Senior EM, Director, VP)
- Balance Pakistan-based vs. international remote workforce (target 70% Pakistan, 30% international by Year 3)
- Drive organizational efficiency: maintain revenue-per-employee >$200K by Year 3

### 4. Culture & Employee Engagement
- Scale high-performance culture from founding team (5-10) to 100+ employees without losing engineering excellence focus
- Drive employee engagement scores: target >80% eNPS by Year 2, >85% by Year 3
- Implement culture programs: values definition, cultural onboarding, team rituals, recognition programs
- Build inclusive culture across Pakistan and international remote teams (addressing time zones, cultural differences, communication norms)
- Champion Artifact Virtual's cultural principles: Precision, Resilience, Ownership, Speed
- Drive quarterly engagement surveys and act on feedback (response rate >85%, action plans implemented within 30 days)
- Foster technical excellence culture: hackathons, innovation time, conference sponsorships, open-source contributions
- Manage workplace environment in Islamabad HQ and remote work policies

### 5. Performance Management & Development
- Implement performance management system: OKRs (quarterly), performance reviews (semi-annual), continuous feedback
- Design calibration process for fair, consistent performance evaluations across departments
- Create performance improvement plans (PIPs) with clear success criteria and support systems
- Drive high-performance culture: top 20% performers get 30%+ compensation increases, bottom 10% managed out or developed
- Link performance ratings to compensation: exceeds expectations = 10-20% raises, meets = 5-10%, below = 0-5% or PIP
- Implement 360-degree feedback for leadership roles (Director+)
- Own promotion processes: clear criteria, calibration sessions, transparency in advancement
- Manage performance documentation and ensure compliance with Pakistan labor law

### 6. Learning & Development
- Build L&D programs for technical skills: AI/ML training, cloud certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure), advanced programming
- Create leadership development pipeline: first-time manager training, executive coaching, leadership workshops
- Implement professional development budgets: $2,000-5,000 per employee annually for courses, conferences, certifications
- Sponsor attendance at major conferences: NeurIPS, ICML, KubeCon, AWS re:Invent, local tech events
- Develop internal training programs: lunch-and-learns, tech talks, knowledge sharing sessions
- Create onboarding excellence: 90-day onboarding plans, buddy systems, role-specific training tracks
- Track L&D effectiveness: training hours per employee, skill development metrics, promotion readiness
- Build partnerships with training providers and educational institutions

### 7. Retention & Succession Planning
- Maintain leadership retention >90% (C-suite, VPs, Directors), overall retention >80% annually
- Implement retention programs: career development conversations, promotion pathways, equity refresh grants
- Conduct stay interviews with high performers and at-risk talent (quarterly for critical roles)
- Analyze attrition data: voluntary vs. involuntary, regrettable vs. non-regrettable, flight-risk identification
- Develop succession plans for all critical roles: identify backups for C-suite and VP positions, 80% of Director+ roles have successors
- Create retention risk dashboard: flight risk scores, market competitiveness, engagement data, tenure analysis
- Implement counter-offer strategies when top talent receives external offers
- Build alumni network to maintain relationships with former employees

### 8. HR Compliance & Employment Law
- Ensure 100% compliance with Pakistan labor law: Industrial Relations Act, Factories Act, Payment of Wages Act, EOBI, Social Security
- Navigate international employment law for US/EU employees: employment contracts, tax withholding, benefits compliance
- Manage employment contracts: probation periods (3-6 months), termination clauses, non-compete/non-solicit agreements
- Oversee leave management: annual leave (Pakistan statutory 14 days minimum), sick leave, parental leave policies
- Handle disciplinary actions and terminations: documentation, legal review, severance calculations
- Maintain employee records and HR documentation in compliance with data protection requirements
- Manage workplace safety compliance for Islamabad data center operations
- Coordinate with General Counsel on employment disputes, labor board proceedings, litigation

### 9. HR Technology & Systems
- Implement HRIS (Human Resources Information System): workable solutions for Pakistan + international markets (e.g., BambooHR, Workday, Gusto)
- Deploy ATS (Applicant Tracking System) for recruiting: Greenhouse, Lever, or custom-built solution integrated with Artifact ERP
- Implement performance management system: Lattice, 15Five, Culture Amp, or module within Artifact ERP
- Build HR analytics dashboard: headcount metrics, attrition analysis, time-to-hire, cost-per-hire, diversity metrics
- Integrate HR systems with payroll, benefits administration, equity management (Carta, Pulley)
- Ensure data security and privacy for employee information (Shield256 encryption, access controls)
- Drive HR process automation: onboarding workflows, performance review cycles, compensation reviews
- Manage HR technology budget and vendor relationships

### 10. Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI)
- Drive diversity in hiring: gender diversity targets (40% by Year 3), ethnic diversity, international representation
- Implement inclusive hiring practices: structured interviews, diverse interview panels, bias training for hiring managers
- Create inclusive workplace policies: flexible work arrangements, religious observance accommodation, accessibility
- Build DEI metrics and reporting: diversity by level, pay equity analysis, promotion rates by demographic groups
- Establish employee resource groups (ERGs): women in tech, international employees, early-career professionals
- Partner with diversity-focused organizations and universities to build diverse talent pipelines
- Conduct pay equity audits annually and remediate gaps (target: <5% unexplained pay gap)
- Drive inclusive culture through training, awareness programs, and leadership accountability
