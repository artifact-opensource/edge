# Chief Operating Officer (COO) - Detailed Role Description

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Summary

The Chief Operating Officer is the operational architect who transforms Artifact Virtual's strategic vision into flawless execution. This role demands a unique combination of operational mastery, customer obsession, technical credibility, and leadership excellence to deliver world-class service across Pakistan, US, and EU markets while scaling from startup operations to enterprise-grade infrastructure.

---

## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

### Morning Routine (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM)

**Operational Health Check (45 minutes)**
- Review overnight system health: uptime, incidents, performance metrics across all infrastructure
- Check customer escalations, support tickets, and health score changes
- Monitor data center environmental systems (power, cooling, security)
- Review operational dashboards: SLA compliance, customer onboarding status, QA metrics
- Identify critical issues requiring immediate attention

**Incident & Issue Review (30 minutes)**
- Review all incidents from previous 24 hours and resolution status
- Check open P0/P1 incidents and coordinate response if needed
- Review incident trends and patterns for systemic issues
- Ensure post-incident reviews scheduled for major incidents
- Update stakeholders on any customer-impacting issues

**Team Sync & Daily Standup (30 minutes)**
- Daily standup with operational leadership (Customer Success, QA, Operations, Facilities)
- Address immediate blockers and resource needs
- Coordinate on customer escalations and critical issues
- Align on day's priorities and critical decisions
- Review team capacity and workload distribution

**Strategic Operations Time (45 minutes)**
- Deep work on operational strategy and process improvements
- Review operational metrics trends and patterns
- Long-term capacity planning and infrastructure roadmap
- Vendor relationship management and contract reviews
- Operational hiring and team development planning

### Midday Activities (9:00 AM - 2:00 PM)

**Customer Success Operations (Variable)**
- Customer business reviews for strategic accounts
- Customer escalation handling and executive problem-solving
- Customer onboarding oversight and quality checks
- Customer health review meetings with CS leadership
- Customer feedback analysis and action planning
- Reference customer development and case study coordination

**Cross-Functional Coordination (Variable)**
- Product-Operations alignment meetings on roadmap and launches
- Engineering-Operations coordination on deployments and changes
- Sales-Operations handoff meetings for new customer onboarding
- Finance-Operations budget reviews and operational planning
- Marketing-Operations coordination on customer stories and case studies
- Executive team meetings on strategic initiatives

**Quality & Process Management (Ongoing)**
- QA strategy reviews and testing framework updates
- Process improvement initiatives and optimization projects
- SOP reviews and documentation updates
- Operational audit preparation and compliance reviews
- Root cause analysis sessions for recurring issues
- Quality metrics review and continuous improvement planning

### Afternoon Focus (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM)

**Data Center & Facilities Operations (Variable)**
- Data center operations reviews and capacity planning
- Facilities walkthroughs and infrastructure inspections (weekly)
- Vendor management meetings and performance reviews
- Equipment procurement and infrastructure upgrade planning
- Power and cooling optimization initiatives
- Physical security and access control reviews
- Disaster recovery testing and business continuity planning

**Team Leadership & Development (Variable)**
- 1-on-1 meetings with direct reports (weekly rotation)
- Team performance reviews and coaching sessions
- Operational hiring interviews and candidate evaluations
- Team training and capability development sessions
- Career development discussions and succession planning
- Team building activities and engagement initiatives
- Skip-level meetings with high-potential operational staff

**Operational Planning & Strategy (Variable)**
- Operational roadmap development and refinement
- Capacity planning for infrastructure and team scaling
- Process automation initiatives and tool evaluation
- Operational cost optimization projects
- Vendor strategy and partnership development
- Business continuity and disaster recovery planning
- Operational risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Evening Work (6:00 PM - 8:00 PM)

**Communication & Coordination**
- Email and communication catch-up
- CEO/executive team updates on operational status
- Customer communication for strategic accounts
- Vendor and partner follow-ups
- US/EU time zone coordination meetings
- Documentation and operational reporting

**Continuous Improvement & Learning**
- Operations best practices research
- Competitive operational intelligence
- Technology and tool evaluation
- Operational framework development
- Industry trend analysis (AIOps, automation, etc.)
- Professional development and learning

**On-Call & Incident Management**
- Availability for critical operational incidents
- Executive escalation coordination
- Crisis communication and stakeholder management
- Major incident command when required

### Weekly Cadence

**Monday: Operational Planning & Review**
- Week kickoff with operational leadership team
- Review previous week's operational metrics and incidents
- Set weekly priorities and commitments
- Customer health review and escalation planning
- Capacity and resource allocation for the week

**Tuesday: Customer Success Focus**
- Customer success metrics review
- Customer escalation deep-dives
- Customer onboarding pipeline review
- Customer feedback and NPS analysis
- Strategic account planning sessions

**Wednesday: Quality & Process Excellence**
- QA metrics and quality trends review
- Process improvement project updates
- SOP and documentation reviews
- Incident trend analysis and prevention planning
- Compliance and audit readiness assessment

**Thursday: Infrastructure & Operations**
- Data center operations review
- Facilities and infrastructure planning
- Vendor performance reviews
- Capacity planning and forecasting
- Technical operations coordination with CTO organization

**Friday: Team & Strategic Planning**
- Operational team development and coaching
- Strategic operations planning for next quarter
- Vendor negotiations and contract reviews
- Week retrospective and continuous improvement
- Planning for upcoming week and month

## Decision-Making Authority

### Full Authority (No Approval Required)
- Operational decisions and process changes
- Customer escalation resolution and service recovery
- Operational hiring below Director level
- Vendor selection and contracts <$25K annually
- Operational tools and software purchases <$10K
- Process improvement initiatives and projects
- SOP creation and updates
- Operational team structure and assignments
- Training and development programs
- Incident response and resolution decisions

### Requires CEO Approval
- Operational budget and annual planning
- Director-level and above hiring
- Major vendor contracts >$100K annually
- Significant process changes affecting product or engineering
- Data center expansion or major infrastructure investments
- Business continuity plan major changes
- Operational structure changes affecting other departments
- SLA changes for existing customers

### Requires CEO Notification
- Customer escalations for accounts >$500K ARR
- P0 incidents affecting multiple customers
- Major vendor changes or terminations
- Operational risk events requiring board awareness
- Compliance audit findings (major)
- Significant operational metrics misses
- Team changes affecting executive leadership
- Cost overruns >10% in any category

## Success Profile

### Traits of Successful COOs in This Role

**The Operational Perfectionist**
- Obsessed with operational excellence and reliability
- Cannot tolerate sloppy processes or preventable failures
- Drives continuous improvement relentlessly
- Sets and maintains high operational standards

**The Customer Champion**
- Authentic passion for customer success and satisfaction
- Makes decisions through customer impact lens
- Builds strong relationships with strategic accounts
- Translates customer feedback into operational action

**The Builder-Executor**
- Designs scalable operational systems and processes
- Executes flawlessly on operational commitments
- Builds teams that deliver consistent excellence
- Balances building for future with executing today

**The Calm Commander**
- Maintains composure during operational crises
- Provides clear direction and decisive leadership under pressure
- Communicates effectively during stressful situations
- Learns from incidents and implements improvements

**The Technical Operator**
- Understands infrastructure and technical operations deeply
- Earns respect from engineering and technical teams
- Can dive into technical details when needed
- Balances technical depth with operational breadth

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Operational Pitfalls:**
- Over-optimizing for cost at expense of reliability or customer experience
- Scaling infrastructure too fast or too slow relative to demand
- Neglecting operational documentation and knowledge transfer
- Not investing in automation and operational tooling early enough

**Customer Success Pitfalls:**
- Treating all customers the same without segmentation
- Reactive rather than proactive customer success approach
- Not building customer feedback loops into operations
- Allowing customer escalations to reach executive level too often

**Team & Culture Pitfalls:**
- Not building operational bench strength and succession
- Creating hero culture instead of systematic excellence
- Burning out operational teams with unsustainable on-call
- Not investing in operational training and development

**Process Pitfalls:**
- Over-processing with bureaucracy that slows execution
- Under-processing with chaos and inconsistency
- Not adapting processes as company scales
- Building processes around current team instead of future scale

## Onboarding & First 90 Days

### First 30 Days: Assess & Understand

**Week 1-2: Operational Assessment**
- Shadow operational teams (Customer Success, QA, Operations, Facilities)
- Review all operational metrics, dashboards, and reporting
- Understand current operational processes and SOPs
- Assess data center facility and infrastructure
- Review customer health, escalations, and support backlog
- Meet with top 10 customers and understand their experience

**Week 3-4: Stakeholder Alignment**
- 1-on-1s with all direct reports and key operational staff
- Cross-functional meetings with CTO, CFO, Sales, Engineering, Product
- Understand operational pain points and bottlenecks
- Review operational budget and resource allocation
- Assess team capabilities and identify gaps
- Develop initial operational priorities and quick wins

### Days 31-60: Plan & Align

**Week 5-6: Operational Strategy**
- Present operational assessment and priorities to CEO
- Develop 90-day operational plan with key initiatives
- Establish operational KPIs and measurement systems
- Begin operational hiring for critical gaps
- Implement quick wins to build credibility and momentum
- Launch weekly operational review cadence

**Week 7-8: Execute & Build**
- Execute on high-priority operational improvements
- Implement customer success programs and metrics
- Establish operational communication rhythms
- Begin process documentation and SOP updates
- Launch operational training programs
- Build relationships with key vendors and partners

### Days 61-90: Scale & Optimize

**Week 9-10: Operational Transformation**
- Roll out major operational improvements and programs
- Scale operational team with key hires
- Implement operational tools and systems
- Launch customer success initiatives and programs
- Establish operational excellence culture and practices
- Drive measurable improvements in key operational metrics

**Week 11-12: Sustainability & Planning**
- Present 90-day results to CEO and executive team
- Develop 12-month operational roadmap
- Establish operational budgets and plans
- Build operational scorecards and dashboards
- Cement operational rhythms and cadences
- Plan for next phase of operational scaling
