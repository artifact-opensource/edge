# Chief Operating Officer (COO) - Roles & Responsibilities

**Classification:** Internal - Confidential  
**Last Updated:** February 2026  
**Organization:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Position Overview

The Chief Operating Officer (COO) serves as the operational backbone of Artifact Virtual, responsible for translating strategic vision into operational excellence across all business functions. This role ensures world-class execution across customer success, quality assurance, business operations, facilities management, and service delivery for AI/ML infrastructure, data center operations, and enterprise software platforms.

**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**Direct Reports:** VP Customer Success, VP Quality Assurance, VP Business Operations, Director of Facilities, Operations Managers  
**Location:** Islamabad, Pakistan (with coordination across US/EU virtual operations)

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Operational Excellence & Service Delivery
- Own end-to-end operational execution across Pakistan, US, and EU markets
- Ensure 99.99% uptime SLA achievement for data center and infrastructure services
- Drive operational efficiency and process optimization across all business units
- Establish and maintain operational frameworks, procedures, and best practices
- Implement and monitor operational metrics and KPIs across all departments
- Lead continuous improvement initiatives using Lean, Six Sigma, and agile methodologies
- Ensure seamless execution of service delivery commitments to all customer segments
- Manage operational budgets and cost optimization initiatives

### 2. Customer Success & Experience
- Lead customer success strategy from onboarding through renewal and expansion
- Build and scale customer success operations supporting 10-25 customers Year 1, scaling to 250-400 by Year 5
- Design and implement customer onboarding programs for Local, Virtual, and Cloud operational models
- Drive customer satisfaction, retention, and Net Promoter Score (NPS) targets (70+ by Year 3)
- Establish customer health monitoring systems and proactive engagement programs
- Lead quarterly business reviews with strategic accounts
- Create customer feedback loops to inform product and service improvements
- Manage escalation procedures and executive-level customer issue resolution
- Build customer advocacy programs and reference customer relationships

### 3. Quality Assurance & Operational Risk Management
- Own quality assurance strategy for all products, services, and operations
- Establish and maintain quality standards across infrastructure, software, and services
- Design and implement comprehensive testing frameworks (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Lead incident management, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement processes
- Ensure compliance with SLAs, operational agreements, and service commitments
- Drive operational risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Implement business continuity and disaster recovery programs
- Oversee security operations coordination (working with CISO)
- Maintain operational audit readiness for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GRC compliance

### 4. Data Center & Facilities Operations
- Oversee data center operations in Islamabad with 99.99% uptime targets
- Manage facilities including power systems, cooling, physical security, and infrastructure
- Lead vendor management for utilities, equipment, maintenance, and supplies
- Ensure redundancy in power (UPS, generators), cooling, and network connectivity
- Drive capacity planning and infrastructure scaling to support growth
- Implement environmental monitoring and sustainable operations practices
- Coordinate physical security, access control, and facility compliance
- Manage relationships with colocation partners and infrastructure providers
- Oversee facility expansion projects and capital improvements

### 5. Process Design & Operational Efficiency
- Design scalable processes to support growth from startup to enterprise scale
- Implement operational systems and tools (ERP, CRM, ticketing, monitoring)
- Drive automation and digitization of operational workflows
- Establish standard operating procedures (SOPs) across all operational functions
- Lead process documentation, training, and knowledge management initiatives
- Implement operational analytics and reporting frameworks
- Drive cost efficiency without compromising service quality
- Optimize resource utilization across teams and infrastructure

### 6. Cross-Functional Coordination & Integration
- Serve as primary operational liaison between Product, Engineering, Sales, and Support
- Coordinate go-to-market execution for product launches across operational models
- Align operational capabilities with product roadmap and customer commitments
- Lead cross-functional incident response and problem resolution
- Drive operational planning for peak seasons, launches, and special events
- Coordinate with Finance on operational budgeting and financial planning
- Partner with HR on operational hiring, training, and workforce planning
- Collaborate with Legal on operational contracts, compliance, and risk

### 7. Vendor & Procurement Operations Management
- Lead vendor selection, negotiation, and relationship management
- Manage procurement operations for equipment, services, and supplies
- Establish vendor performance management and SLA enforcement
- Drive vendor risk assessment and due diligence processes
- Negotiate cost-effective contracts while maintaining quality standards
- Build strategic partnerships with key infrastructure and service providers
- Manage vendor escalations and dispute resolution
- Ensure vendor compliance with security, privacy, and operational requirements

### 8. Team Building & Operational Leadership
- Build and scale operational teams in Pakistan (primary) with remote US/EU coordination
- Recruit, develop, and retain world-class operations professionals
- Establish operational team structures, roles, and career paths
- Drive operational culture of excellence, accountability, and customer obsession
- Implement operational training programs and capability development
- Foster collaboration between Customer Success, QA, Operations, and Facilities teams
- Lead performance management for operational teams
- Develop operational leadership bench strength and succession planning
