**Report Date:** 2026-02-01

## Executive Summary

The United States represents the world's largest and most mature data center market, with an estimated market size of $60+ billion in 2026. The market is characterized by strong demand for ML/AI infrastructure, increasing edge computing requirements, and growing emphasis on sustainability. This analysis provides a comprehensive view of market opportunities for Artifact Virtual's virtual operations targeting US clients.

### Key Findings

- **Market Size:** US data center market valued at $60-65 billion (2026)
- **Growth Rate:** 8-10% CAGR through 2030
- **ML/AI Segment:** Fastest growing at 25-30% CAGR
- **Primary Hubs:** Northern Virginia, Silicon Valley, Dallas, Chicago, New York
- **Key Trends:** Edge computing, AI/ML workloads, sustainability, hybrid cloud
- **Opportunity:** High demand for specialized R&D and ML infrastructure services

## 2. Geographic Distribution

### 2.1 Major Data Center Markets

**Northern Virginia (Ashburn - "Data Center Alley")**
- Largest data center market in the world
- 1,800+ MW of capacity
- Hub for AWS, Microsoft, Google
- Excellent connectivity and fiber infrastructure
- Average colocation pricing: $90-150/kW/month

**Silicon Valley (San Jose, Santa Clara)**
- Tech industry concentration
- 600+ MW capacity
- Premium pricing: $150-250/kW/month
- High-value customers
- Innovation hub

**Dallas/Fort Worth**
- Central US location
- 500+ MW capacity
- Competitive pricing: $80-120/kW/month
- Growing market
- Good fiber connectivity

**Chicago**
- Midwest hub
- 400+ MW capacity
- Major financial services presence
- Pricing: $85-130/kW/month
- Key interconnection point

**Phoenix**
- Emerging market
- 350+ MW capacity
- Lower costs: $70-110/kW/month
- Growing tech presence
- Renewable energy focus

**Other Notable Markets**
- New York/New Jersey (financial services)
- Seattle (tech, cloud providers)
- Los Angeles (entertainment, content)
- Atlanta (telecommunications hub)
- Portland (renewable energy focus)

### 2.2 Market Concentration

- Top 5 markets: 60% of total capacity
- Top 10 markets: 80% of total capacity
- Tier 2 markets growing faster than Tier 1
- Edge locations proliferating in smaller cities

## 7. Pricing Analysis

### 7.1 Colocation Pricing

**Rack Colocation**
- Full rack (42U): $1,500-3,500/month
- Half rack (21U): $800-1,800/month
- Quarter rack: $400-900/month
- Power (per kW): $90-200/month
- Premium markets command 30-50% higher prices

**Cage/Suite Pricing**
- Small cage (200-500 sq ft): $3,000-8,000/month
- Medium suite (500-1,500 sq ft): $10,000-30,000/month
- Large suite (1,500+ sq ft): $30,000-100,000+/month
- Power costs additional

**Cross-connects and Interconnection**
- Physical cross-connect: $300-600/month
- 10Gbps port: $1,000-2,500/month
- 100Gbps port: $5,000-15,000/month
- Cloud on-ramps: $500-2,000/month

### 7.2 Cloud and Virtual Services

**Compute Pricing (indicative)**
- CPU instance (8 vCPU, 32GB RAM): $200-400/month
- GPU instance (NVIDIA A100): $3,000-5,000/month
- High-memory instance: $300-600/month
- Spot/preemptible instances: 60-80% discount

**Storage Pricing**
- Block storage: $0.08-0.15/GB/month
- Object storage: $0.02-0.04/GB/month
- Archive storage: $0.004-0.01/GB/month
- Data transfer: $0.05-0.12/GB (egress)

**Specialized Services**
- ML training: $5-20 per training hour
- Inference hosting: $0.50-2.00 per million inferences
- Big data analytics: Variable by usage
- Managed databases: $100-1,000+/month

### 7.3 Managed Services

**Professional Services**
- Network management: $1,000-5,000/month
- Security management: $2,000-10,000/month
- Application management: $3,000-15,000/month
- Migration services: $50,000-500,000 per project
- Consulting: $150-400/hour

**Support Tiers**
- Basic (business hours): Included
- Enhanced (24/7): $500-2,000/month
- Premium (dedicated team): $5,000-20,000/month
