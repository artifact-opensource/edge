### High Priority Risks (Mitigation Required)

1. **Nayatel Competition in Islamabad**
   - Risk: Strong local brand and relationships
   - Mitigation: Specialize in ML/AI, differentiate technology, win on innovation

2. **Power Reliability Issues**
   - Risk: Grid instability, frequent outages
   - Mitigation: Comprehensive backup (generators + UPS + renewables)

3. **HEKTOR Development/Delivery**
   - Risk: Technology delays or performance issues
   - Mitigation: Phased development, continuous testing, fallback plans

4. **Government Customer Concentration**
   - Risk: 30-35% revenue from government segment
   - Mitigation: Diversify across financial services, enterprises, startups

### Medium Priority Risks (Monitor)

1. **Currency Volatility:** PKR fluctuations affecting costs
2. **Hyperscaler Entry:** AWS/Azure building Pakistan regions (unlikely near-term)
3. **Talent Shortage:** Competition for ML/AI engineers
4. **Regulatory Changes:** Evolving compliance landscape

### Low Priority Risks

1. **Market Demand:** Multiple growing segments, low risk
2. **Technology Evolution:** Continuous R&D investment mitigates
3. **Competition Copying:** Technology moats and speed protect
