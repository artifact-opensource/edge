## Focus: Islamabad and Surrounding Regions

**Report Date:** 2026-02-01  
**Classification:** Confidential  
**Prepared For:** Artifact Virtual (SMC-Private) Limited

---

## Executive Summary

Pakistan's data center and IT services market is emerging rapidly, driven by government digitalization initiatives, growing tech sector, and increasing internet penetration. The Islamabad Capital Territory and surrounding regions (Rawalpindi, Wah) represent the premier technology hub in Pakistan, hosting government institutions, major IT companies, and a growing startup ecosystem. This analysis identifies substantial opportunities for Artifact Virtual's local data center operations.

### Key Findings

- **National Market Size:** PKR 80-100 billion (~$280-350 million USD) in 2026
- **Growth Rate:** 15-20% CAGR through 2030
- **Islamabad Region:** 35-40% of national IT market
- **Key Drivers:** Digital Pakistan, government IT, financial sector growth, startup ecosystem
- **Competitive Landscape:** Limited modern data center capacity, dominated by telcos
- **Opportunity:** High demand for reliable, secure, and specialized infrastructure
- **Cost Advantage:** 60-70% lower operational costs vs. Western markets

## 7. Infrastructure Analysis

### 7.1 Power and Energy

**Current State**
- Industrial electricity: PKR 15-22/kWh
- Power stability improving
- Load shedding reduced but not eliminated
- Industrial zones prioritized
- Summer demand peaks

**Requirements for Data Center**
- Dual power feeds essential
- UPS systems mandatory
- Generator backup (3-5 days fuel)
- Solar power supplementation opportunity
- Power factor correction
- Monitoring and management systems

**Cost Implications**
- Backup power: PKR 40-60/kWh (diesel)
- Solar investment: PKR 100-150M for 1MW
- UPS systems: PKR 50-100M
- Energy costs: 40-50% of operational expenses

### 7.2 Connectivity

**International Connectivity**
- Multiple submarine cables via Karachi
- TWA (Transworld Associates) landing station
- PEACE Cable
- SMW5, SMW4
- Latency to Middle East: 50-80ms
- Latency to Europe: 120-150ms
- Latency to US West Coast: 250-300ms

**Domestic Backbone**
- PTCL fiber network
- Multiple ISP networks
- Good metro fiber in Islamabad
- Microwave backup links
- Expanding coverage

**Last Mile**
- Multiple ISP options
- Fiber availability in industrial areas
- Diverse path options
- Peering facilities limited

**Bandwidth Costs**
- International: $3-8 per Mbps
- Domestic: PKR 50,000-200,000/Gbps/month
- Decreasing costs trend
- Better pricing for data centers

### 7.3 Real Estate

**Land Costs (Islamabad/Rawalpindi)**
- I-9 Industrial: PKR 25,000-40,000/sq yard
- I-10 Area: PKR 30,000-50,000/sq yard
- Rawalpindi Industrial: PKR 15,000-30,000/sq yard
- Required: 2,000-5,000 sq yards for initial facility

**Building Costs**
- Data center construction: PKR 75,000-150,000/sq ft
- Standard construction: PKR 3,000-5,000/sq ft
- Specialized requirements increase costs
- Timeline: 12-18 months for purpose-built

**Rental Options**
- Industrial warehouse: PKR 150-300/sq ft/month
- Commercial space: PKR 300-500/sq ft/month
- Retrofit possible for initial phase
- Limited purpose-built DC space available

### 7.4 Environmental Factors

**Climate**
- Temperature range: 0-45°C
- Hot summers (May-August)
- Mild winters (December-February)
- Moderate humidity
- Cooling requirements: 6-9 months/year
- Free cooling possible 3-4 months

**Cooling Costs**
- HVAC operational: 30-40% of power consumption
- Chiller systems required
- Evaporative cooling supplementation
- Aisle containment beneficial
- Target PUE: 1.4-1.6 achievable

**Natural Disaster Risk**
- Seismic zone 2B (moderate risk)
- Earthquake-resistant design required
- Flood risk: Low (elevated terrain)
- Weather events: Rare
- Overall: Relatively stable environment
