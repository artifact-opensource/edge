## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company desires to engage the services of the Executive as Chief Executive Officer to lead the organization's strategic direction, operational excellence, and stakeholder value creation;

WHEREAS, the Executive possesses the qualifications, experience, and capabilities required to fulfill the responsibilities of Chief Executive Officer;

WHEREAS, both parties desire to establish the terms and conditions of this executive engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1: APPOINTMENT AND TERM

### 1.1 Position

The Company hereby appoints the Executive to serve as Chief Executive Officer, and the Executive hereby accepts such appointment, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

### 1.2 Reporting Structure             

The Executive shall report directly to the Board of Directors of the Company ("the Board").

### 1.3 Term of Employment

**Initial Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of three (3) years ("Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions herein.

**Renewal:** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

### 1.4 Commencement

The Executive's active service shall commence on: [Start Date]

---

## ARTICLE 2: DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

### 2.1 Primary Responsibilities

The Executive shall:

a) Serve as the principal executive officer of the Company with full authority and responsibility for the management and direction of the Company's business and affairs, subject to the oversight of the Board;

b) Develop and execute the Company's strategic vision, mission, and long-term objectives;

c) Lead the executive management team and ensure organizational alignment with strategic goals;

d) Represent the Company to key stakeholders including investors, partners, customers, and regulatory bodies;

e) Ensure the Company maintains the highest standards of corporate governance, ethics, and compliance;

f) Report regularly to the Board on the Company's performance, strategic progress, and material matters;

g) Safeguard the Company's assets, reputation, and stakeholder interests;

h) Build and maintain a high-performance organizational culture;

i) Oversee financial performance and ensure fiscal responsibility;

j) Drive innovation and maintain competitive positioning.

### 2.2 Standard of Performance

The Executive agrees to:

a) Devote substantially all professional time, attention, and energy to the business of the Company;

b) Perform duties faithfully, diligently, and to the best of the Executive's abilities;

c) Act in the best interests of the Company and its stakeholders at all times;

d) Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and Company policies;

e) Maintain the highest standards of professional conduct and ethical behavior.

### 2.3 Board Service

The Executive shall serve as a member of the Board of Directors during the term of employment, subject to applicable governance requirements and shareholder approval where required.

### 2.4 Location

The Executive's primary place of work shall be [Primary Location], with travel as required to fulfill the responsibilities of the position.

## ARTICLE 3: COMPENSATION

### 3.1 Base Salary

The Executive shall receive an annual base salary of [Amount] ("Base Salary"), payable in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices, less applicable withholdings and deductions.

### 3.2 Annual Review

The Base Salary shall be reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee of the Board and may be adjusted based on performance, market conditions, and other relevant factors. Any increase shall not reduce the Base Salary below the amount set forth herein.

### 3.5 Signing Bonus

Upon execution of this Agreement and commencement of employment, the Executive shall receive a one-time signing bonus of [Amount], subject to applicable withholdings.

## ARTICLE 5: TERMINATION

### 5.1 Termination by the Company for Cause

The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately for Cause. "Cause" shall mean:

a) Material breach of this Agreement or fiduciary duties;
b) Gross negligence or willful misconduct in performance of duties;
c) Conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or crime involving moral turpitude;
d) Fraud, embezzlement, or misappropriation of Company assets;
e) Material violation of Company policies or applicable law;
f) Failure to perform duties after written notice and reasonable opportunity to cure.

### 5.2 Termination by the Company Without Cause

The Company may terminate this Agreement without Cause upon sixty (60) days written notice to the Executive.

### 5.3 Termination by the Executive

The Executive may terminate this Agreement:

a) **For Good Reason:** Upon thirty (30) days written notice following occurrence of Good Reason (as defined below) and failure of the Company to cure within such period;

b) **Without Good Reason:** Upon ninety (90) days written notice.

**Good Reason** shall mean:
- Material diminution in authority, duties, or responsibilities
- Material reduction in Base Salary or target bonus opportunity
- Material breach of this Agreement by the Company
- Required relocation of more than fifty (50) miles

### 5.4 Termination Due to Death or Disability

This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon the Executive's death or Disability. "Disability" means the Executive's inability to perform essential job functions for a period of one hundred eighty (180) consecutive days due to physical or mental impairment.

---

## ARTICLE 6: SEVERANCE

### 6.1 Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason

If the Company terminates this Agreement without Cause, or if the Executive terminates for Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled to:

a) Accrued but unpaid Base Salary through the termination date;
b) Pro-rated Annual Incentive for the year of termination;
c) Severance payment equal to [Number] months of Base Salary;
d) COBRA premium continuation for [Number] months;
e) Accelerated vesting of equity awards as provided in applicable award agreements;
f) Outplacement services up to [Amount].

### 6.2 Termination for Cause or Without Good Reason

If the Company terminates for Cause, or if the Executive terminates without Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled only to accrued but unpaid Base Salary through the termination date.

### 6.3 Release Requirement

Payment of severance benefits under Section 6.1 shall be conditioned upon the Executive's execution and non-revocation of a general release of claims in a form acceptable to the Company.

### 6.4 Change in Control

In the event of a Change in Control (as defined in the Company's LTIP) followed within twenty-four (24) months by termination without Cause or resignation for Good Reason, the Executive shall be entitled to enhanced severance of [Number] months of Base Salary plus [Number] times Target Bonus.

## ARTICLE 7: RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

### 7.1 Confidentiality

The Executive agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information (as defined below) during and after employment, and shall not disclose or use such information except as required in the performance of duties or as authorized by the Company.

**Confidential Information** includes all non-public information relating to the Company's business, operations, strategies, finances, technology, customers, partners, and employees.

### 7.2 Non-Competition

During employment and for a period of [Number] months following termination, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, manage, or participate in any business that competes with the Company's business in [Geographic Scope].

### 7.3 Non-Solicitation

During employment and for a period of [Number] months following termination, the Executive shall not:

a) Solicit or attempt to solicit any employee of the Company to leave employment;
b) Solicit or attempt to solicit any customer, client, or partner of the Company.

### 7.4 Intellectual Property

All inventions, discoveries, developments, and work product created by the Executive during employment and related to Company business shall be the sole property of the Company.

### 7.5 Return of Property

Upon termination, the Executive shall promptly return all Company property, documents, and Confidential Information in the Executive's possession.

## ARTICLE 8: INDEMNIFICATION

### 8.1 Indemnification

The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law against all expenses, liabilities, and losses arising from the Executive's service to the Company, provided such actions were taken in good faith and in the reasonable belief that such actions were in the Company's best interests.

### 8.2 Directors and Officers Insurance

The Company shall maintain Directors and Officers liability insurance coverage during the Executive's employment and for a period of six (6) years thereafter, with coverage amounts consistent with industry standards.
