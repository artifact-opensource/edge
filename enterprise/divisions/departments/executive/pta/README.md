## PTA CVAS Licensing

### What is CVAS?
Content/Value Added Services (CVAS) licensing is required by PTA for companies providing:
- Digital content services
- Value-added telecom services
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platforms accessible over telecom networks
- Cloud-based services delivered to end users in Pakistan
- AI/ML services with telecom integration

### Why Artifact Virtual Needs CVAS
- SaaS product delivery to Pakistani market
- Cloud infrastructure services
- AI/ML platform services
- Data services and API delivery
