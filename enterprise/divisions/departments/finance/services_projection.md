## Overview

This projection models revenue from Artifact Virtual's **7 service categories** with 20+ individual offerings. Revenue assumptions are based on the services catalog pricing, infrastructure capacity plans, and regulatory timeline from [03_SERVICES-CATALOG.md](../../../../docs/investor-profile/03_SERVICES-CATALOG.md) and [10_REGULATORY-STRATEGY.md](../../../../docs/investor-profile/10_REGULATORY-STRATEGY.md).

**Revenue Model:** Monthly subscriptions, hourly usage, project-based fees, retainers

**Key Dependency:** PTA CVAS license (required for hosting/VPS services) — target Q2-Q3 2026
