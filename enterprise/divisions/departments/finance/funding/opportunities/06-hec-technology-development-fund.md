## Executive Summary

The HEC Technology Development Fund (TDF) provides PKR 5-14 million grants to bridge academic research and industry commercialization. With HEKTOR's spectral similarity search technology having strong research foundations and clear market applications, this represents an excellent pathway for establishing academic partnerships while securing non-dilutive funding for technical validation and prototype development.

**Key Opportunity Factors:**
- Grant size: PKR 5-14 million (~$17,500-$49,000 USD)

- Mandated university partnership strengthens talent pipeline
- 238+ projects funded, 25 patents granted
- Extended until June 2027
- Strong alignment with AI/data science priorities

## Program Overview

### Mission & Scope

The Technology Development Fund aims to transform university research into market-ready products and services that address Pakistan's socio-economic challenges. The program emphasizes:

- **Applied research commercialization** - Moving from prototype to market
- **Industry-academia collaboration** - Required consortium structure
- **Technology transfer** - IP development and licensing
- **Startup formation** - 18 startups emerged from 238 projects
- **Publication & patents** - 116 patent filings, 300+ publications

### Funding Structure

| **Component** | **Details** |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Grant Amount** | PKR 5-14 million per project (up to PKR 14M) |
| **Duration** | 18-36 months (project-dependent) |
| **Funding Type** | Non-dilutive grant |
| **Co-funding** | Industry partner contribution required |
| **IP Ownership** | Retained by university/industry consortium |
| **Success Fee** | None - full grant funding |
