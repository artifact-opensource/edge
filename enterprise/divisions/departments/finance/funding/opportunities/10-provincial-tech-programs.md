## Executive Summary

Pakistan's four provinces—Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), and Balochistan—operate technology development programs through provincial IT boards, education departments, and entrepreneurship initiatives. While generally **smaller in scale than federal programs** (grants of PKR 1-10M vs. federal PKR 10-50M), provincial programs offer:

- **Faster access** (less bureaucracy than federal level)
- **Regional partnerships** (provincial government agencies as customers)
- **Incubation support** (workspace, mentorship, networking)
- **Skills development collaboration** (access to trained workforce)

**Key Opportunities:**
1. **Punjab IT Labs (PITB):** Plan9 incubation, tech grants (PKR 2-10M)
2. **Sindh Startup Support:** NIC Karachi, entrepreneurship grants (PKR 1-5M)
3. **KP IT Board (KPITB):** Digital skills programs, e-governance partnerships
4. **Balochistan Education Endowment:** Small grants (PKR 500K-2M), underserved market access

**Strategic Value:** Provincial programs are **complementary** to federal funding (PSF, Ignite, NAIF), providing ecosystem access, pilot customers, and workforce development rather than primary capitalization.

## Punjab: Largest Tech Ecosystem

### 1. Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)

**Overview:**
- **Established:** 2006 (Pakistan's oldest and most mature provincial IT board)
- **Mission:** Digital transformation of Punjab government, startup ecosystem development
- **Budget:** PKR 5-10 billion annually (government IT projects + startup programs)
- **Geographic Reach:** Lahore (HQ), Faisalabad, Multan, Rawalpindi

**Key Programs for Startups:**

#### A. Plan9 (Tech Incubator) ⭐⭐⭐

**Description:**
- Pakistan's **largest and most successful tech incubator** (since 2012)
- 6-month cohort-based program (2 cohorts annually)
- Provides: Workspace, mentorship, PKR 1-2M grant, investor access, legal support

**Sectors:**
- Software/SaaS (primary focus)
- Fintech, e-commerce, edtech, healthtech
- AI/ML startups (growing focus)

**Artifact Alignment: 85%**

✓ **Strong Fit:**
- SaaS category (HEKTOR platform)
- Healthtech angle (medical imaging AI)
- Lahore location (Punjab capital, talent hub)
- Export-oriented startups prioritized

! **Considerations:**
- Designed for earlier-stage startups (pre-revenue or <PKR 5M revenue)
- Artifact may be "too advanced" if seed funding secured
- 6-month program may conflict with fundraising timeline

**Program Details:**
- **Application:** Open call 2x/year (typically Feb-Mar, Aug-Sep)
- **Selection:** Competitive (100-200 applications, 15-20 accepted per cohort)
- **Grant:** PKR 1-2M equity-free
- **Equity:** 3-5% equity stake (controversial, but standard)
- **Outcomes:** 100+ startups graduated, including Bookme.pk, Dawaai

**Strategic Approach:**
- **Apply if:** Artifact is pre-seed or very early seed (Q1 2026)
- **Skip if:** Seed round already in progress (time cost > value)
- **Alternative:** Leverage Plan9 **network** without formal incubation (attend events, alumni intros)

**Contact:**
- **Website:** [plan9.pitb.gov.pk](https://plan9.pitb.gov.pk)
- **Email:** info@plan9.pitb.gov.pk
- **Address:** Arfa Software Technology Park, Lahore

#### B. PITB Innovation Grants

**Description:**
- Ad-hoc grants for technology projects addressing Punjab government needs
- Focus: E-governance, civic tech, smart cities, digital health, agtech

**Grant Size:** PKR 2-10M (project-dependent)

**Artifact Opportunity:**
- **Proposal:** "AI-Powered Medical Imaging Platform for Punjab Hospitals"
- **Pilot Partners:** Punjab Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), Services Hospital Lahore
- **Use Case:** Deploy HEKTOR for pathology slide similarity search, radiology image analysis
- **Budget:** PKR 8M (infrastructure, integration, training)
- **Expected Outcome:** 50,000+ medical images indexed, AI-assisted diagnosis for 5+ Punjab hospitals

**Alignment: 80%**

✓ Digital health priority  
✓ Government customer (Punjab Health Department)  
! Requires strong health sector connections (CEO/BD lead must cultivate relationships)

**Application Process:**
- No formal open call (relationship-driven)
- Approach: PITB officials, pitch project, submit proposal if interest
- Timeline: 3-6 months (relationship building → proposal → approval)

## Strategic Integration: Provincial + Federal Funding Stack

**Optimal Funding Mix for Artifact Virtual:**

```
Federal Tier (Primary Capitalization):
- Pakistan Startup Fund (PSF): PKR 10M initial + PKR 40M follow-on
- Ignite Tech Innovation Grant: PKR 20M
- National AI Fund (NAIF): PKR 15-30M (pilot projects)
- Venture Capital (Seed): $1M (~PKR 280M)
Total Federal/VC: ~PKR 350-400M ($1.2-1.4M)

Provincial Tier (Ecosystem Access):
- Plan9 or NIC incubation: PKR 1-3M (if pre-seed timing aligns)
- PITB/KPITB Innovation Grant: PKR 6-10M (health/ag pilot projects)
- Sindh Startup Support: PKR 2-5M (if Karachi office)
Total Provincial: PKR 10-20M ($35-70K)

TOTAL FUNDING (Federal + Provincial): PKR 360-420M ($1.25-1.5M)
```

**Strategic Logic:**
- **Federal/VC = core capital** (sustains operations, builds product, scales team)
- **Provincial = market access** (pilot customers, government relationships, workforce development)

## Risk Assessment

### Opportunity Cost Risk (Medium)

! **Time Investment:**
- Provincial programs require significant BD effort (relationship building, proposal writing, bureaucracy)
- May distract from higher-value federal programs or VC fundraising

✓ **Mitigation:** Selective engagement (only high-alignment programs: Plan9, NIC Karachi, PITB health grants)

---

### Geographic Dispersion Risk (Low-Medium)

! **Multi-Provincial Operations:**
- Engaging Punjab + Sindh + KP programs may require offices/presence in 3 cities
- Artifact is early-stage (limited resources for multi-city expansion)

✓ **Mitigation:** Focus on 1-2 provinces (Punjab + Sindh likely optimal)

---

### Small Grant Sizes (Medium)

! **Limited Financial Impact:**
- Provincial grants (PKR 1-10M) much smaller than federal (PKR 10-50M)
- Time-to-value ratio may be poor

✓ **Mitigation:** Pursue provincial grants only if:
  1. Aligned with existing strategy (e.g., Punjab health pilot supports commercial roadmap)
  2. Opens doors to larger opportunities (e.g., PITB grant → Punjab government customer)

**Overall Risk Level:** Low-Medium  
**Mitigation:** **Selective engagement, prioritize federal/VC over provincial**

✓ **Market access** (provincial government customers)
