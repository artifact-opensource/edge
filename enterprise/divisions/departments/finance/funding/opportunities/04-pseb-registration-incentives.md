## 📋 Program Overview

The Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB) is the premier government agency responsible for promoting Pakistan's IT and software industry globally. PSEB registration is **essential for all IT/software companies** in Pakistan seeking to benefit from government incentives, tax exemptions, and export facilitation services.

PSEB provides a comprehensive ecosystem of support including tax benefits, financial incentives, market access, capacity building, and regulatory facilitation. Registration is straightforward, free of cost, and unlocks significant financial advantages.

### **Key Features:**
- **Zero Registration Fee:** Free registration process
- **Tax Benefits:** 0.25% tax rate on export revenues (vs. 29-35% standard corporate tax)
- **Startup Tax Holiday:** 100% tax exemption for 3 years for new companies
- **Foreign Currency Retention:** Retain 50% of export proceeds in foreign currency
- **Rent Subsidy:** 25% rent subsidy for office space (up to PKR 500K/year)
- **Free Services:** Bandwidth subsidies, office space in PSEB facilities
- **Export Support:** Market access, trade missions, buyer-seller meetings
- **Permanent Program:** Not a temporary scheme - benefits continue indefinitely

## $ Financial Benefits & Incentives

### **1. Tax Benefits (Primary Benefit)**

#### **Final Tax Regime (FTR) - 0.25% on Exports**
- **Standard Corporate Tax Rate:** 29-35% of profits
- **PSEB Registered Rate:** 0.25% of export proceeds (Final Tax Regime)
- **Tax Savings Example:**
  - Export revenue: $1,000,000/year
  - Normal tax (at 30%): ~$200K (after expenses)
  - PSEB FTR tax (0.25%): $2,500
  - **Annual Savings: ~$197,500** $

#### **Startup Tax Credit - 100% Exemption**
- **Eligibility:** Companies incorporated after January 1, 2021
- **Duration:** 3 years from date of incorporation
- **Benefit:** 100% exemption from income tax
- **Conditions:**
  - Must be PSEB registered
  - Minimum 75% revenue from exports
  - Operational in Pakistan
  - Proper tax filing and documentation

**For Artifact Virtual:**
- If incorporated after 2021: Qualify for 3-year tax holiday
- If older: Benefit from 0.25% FTR immediately
- **Estimated 3-Year Savings:** $300K-$500K (depending on revenue growth)

### **2. Foreign Currency Retention**

#### **50% Retention Account Facility**
- **Standard Rule:** Export proceeds must be converted to PKR within 180 days
- **PSEB Benefit:** Retain 50% in foreign currency indefinitely
- **Advantage:** 
  - Hedge against PKR depreciation (15-20% annual average)
  - Pay foreign vendors/servers directly in USD
  - Reduce currency conversion costs (2-3%)
  - Maintain USD reserves for international expansion

**Financial Impact:**
- $1M annual exports → $500K retained in USD
- Currency depreciation protection: ~$75K-$100K/year value preservation
- Conversion cost savings: ~$15K-$30K/year

### **3. Rent Subsidy Program**

#### **Office Space Support**
- **Subsidy Rate:** 25% of monthly rent
- **Maximum:** PKR 500,000 per year (~$1,750 USD at 285 PKR/USD)
- **Eligibility:** PSEB-registered companies in designated cities
- **Duration:** Ongoing (renewable annually)
- **Coverage Cities:** Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Multan, Faisalabad

**Application Process:**
1. Provide lease agreement and rent receipts
2. Submit quarterly reimbursement claims to PSEB
3. Receive reimbursement within 30-45 days

**For Artifact Virtual:**
- Annual office rent: PKR 2M (~$7,000) → Subsidy: PKR 500K (~$1,750)
- **Annual Savings:** $1,750

### **4. Bandwidth & Internet Subsidy**

#### **Free/Subsidized Internet**
- **Tier 1 (0-10 employees):** 10 Mbps free bandwidth
- **Tier 2 (11-50 employees):** 20 Mbps free + subsidized upgrade
- **Tier 3 (51+ employees):** 50 Mbps free + subsidized dedicated line
- **Service Providers:** PTCL, NTC (through PSEB coordination)

**For Artifact Virtual (Data Center Operations):**
- Base bandwidth: 50 Mbps free (~$200/month saved)
- Subsidized dedicated fiber: 50% discount on commercial rates
- **Annual Savings:** $2,400 base + potential $6,000-$12,000 on dedicated lines

### **5. Financial Support Programs**

#### **Emerging IT Firms Financing**
- Subsidized loans through PSEB-partner banks
- Interest rate: KIBOR + 2% (vs. commercial KIBOR + 6-8%)
- Loan amounts: PKR 5M - PKR 50M ($17K-$175K)
- Tenure: Up to 5 years
- **Interest Savings:** 4-6% lower than market rates

#### **Technology Upgradation Fund**
- Matching grants for technology purchases
- Up to 30% subsidy on hardware/software
- Maximum: PKR 5M (~$17,500) per year
- Eligible: Servers, development tools, cloud credits

## 📝 Registration Process

### **Step-by-Step Registration (3-4 Weeks)**

#### **Phase 1: Documentation Preparation (Week 1)**

**Required Documents:**
1. **Company Documents:**
   - [ ] Certificate of Incorporation (SECP)
   - [ ] Memorandum & Articles of Association
   - [ ] Form 29 (Directors & Shareholders)
   - [ ] Updated Share Transfer Register

2. **Tax Documents:**
   - [ ] NTN Certificate (National Tax Number)
   - [ ] FBR Registration Certificate
   - [ ] Last tax return (if filed)
   - [ ] Sales Tax Registration (if applicable)

3. **Business Documents:**
   - [ ] Company Profile (2-3 pages)
   - [ ] Business Plan (summary)
   - [ ] Product/Service Description
   - [ ] Export Plan or Evidence of Export Intent

4. **Bank Documents:**
   - [ ] Bank Account Certificate
   - [ ] Bank statements (3-6 months, if available)

5. **Office Documents:**
   - [ ] Lease Agreement or Ownership Proof
   - [ ] Utility Bill (electricity/gas in company name)
   - [ ] Office photos (exterior + interior)

6. **Identity Documents:**
   - [ ] CNIC copies of all directors
   - [ ] Contact information (email, phone, address)

#### **Phase 2: Online Application (Week 2)**

**Application Portal:** https://portal.pseb.org.pk

1. Create account on PSEB portal
2. Fill online application form (30-45 minutes)
3. Upload all required documents (PDF format)
4. Pay registration fee: **PKR 0 (FREE)** ✓
5. Submit application for review

**Application Form Sections:**
- Company Information
- Business Details (products, services, target markets)
- Financial Information (revenue, exports, employees)
- Director Information
- Office Information

#### **Phase 3: PSEB Verification (Week 3)**

**PSEB Process:**
1. Document verification (2-3 days)
2. Physical office inspection (scheduled)
3. Director interview (optional, for large companies)
4. Background checks (FBR, SECP cross-verification)
5. Committee review

**Office Inspection:**
- PSEB official visits registered office
- Verifies physical operations
- Checks IT infrastructure
- Meeting with team/directors
- Takes photos for records

**Preparation Tips:**
- Ensure office is operational during visit
- Have 2-3 team members present
- Display company signage/branding
- Have computers/development environment visible
- Prepare brief presentation about HEKTOR

#### **Phase 4: Approval & Certificate (Week 4)**

1. Approval notification via email/SMS
2. Collection of PSEB Registration Certificate
3. Allocation of PSEB Registration Number
4. Enrollment in PSEB Portal systems
5. Access to benefits and services activated

**Certificate Includes:**
- Unique PSEB Registration Number
- Company details and category
- Validity: Permanent (renewable annually)
- Director signatures and company seal
