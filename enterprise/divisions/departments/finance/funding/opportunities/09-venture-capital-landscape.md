## PSF Co-Investment Strategy (Critical Component)

### Pakistan Startup Fund (PSF) Follow-On Mechanism

**How It Works:**
1. Artifact applies to PSF (Opportunity Document #01)
2. PSF invests initial amount (e.g., PKR 10M / ~$35K)
3. Artifact raises external VC round (e.g., $500K from Sarmayacar)
4. PSF automatically provides **50% follow-on investment** (up to PKR 50M / ~$175K total)
5. Net effect: **PSF matches external VC 1:1** (up to their limit)

**Artifact Seed Round with PSF:**

```
External VC (Sarmayacar): $500K
PSF Follow-On (50% match): $500K
Total: $1M seed round

Benefits:
- Reduces dilution (PSF terms often more founder-friendly)
- Government "stamp of approval" attracts other VCs
- Extends runway (double the capital from external VC raise)
```

**Strategic Sequencing:**

1. **Secure PSF initial investment** (Q1-Q2 2026)
2. **Use PSF traction to approach VCs** (Q2 2026)
   - "We have government backing via PSF"
3. **Close VC lead investment** (Q3 2026)
   - Sarmayacar commits $500K
4. **Trigger PSF follow-on** (Q3 2026)
   - PSF automatically adds $500K (50% match)
5. **Total seed round closed:** $1M (Q3 2026)

**Critical Success Factor:** PSF relationship MUST be prioritized in Q1-Q2 2026

## Risk Assessment

### VC Market Risks (Low-Medium)

! **Market Volatility:**
- VC funding ebbs and flows with global tech cycles
- Pakistan political/economic uncertainty can reduce VC appetite

✓ **Mitigation:** Diversify funding sources (PSF + VCs + grants)

---

### Valuation Risks (Medium)

! **Hardware Discount:**
- VCs often value SaaS companies higher (10x revenue) vs. infrastructure (3-5x revenue)
- Data center capital intensity may reduce valuation multiples

✓ **Mitigation:** Emphasize SaaS component (HEKTOR licensing), frame data center as "cloud service"

---

### Dilution Risks (Medium)

! **Multiple Rounds:**
- Seed (25%) + Series A (25%) + Series B (20%) = 70% total dilution by Series B
- Founders may end up with <30% ownership

✓ **Mitigation:** Negotiate smaller dilution (20% seed, 20% Series A), increase valuation

---

### Competition Risks (Low)

✓ **First-Mover:** Artifact is early in Pakistan AI infrastructure space  
✓ **Network Effects:** Early customers create switching costs  
! **International Giants:** Pinecone, Weaviate may enter Pakistan (unlikely near-term)

**Overall Risk Level:** Low-Medium  
**Mitigation:** Execute fast (first-mover advantage), build defensible moat (government partnerships, customer lock-in)
