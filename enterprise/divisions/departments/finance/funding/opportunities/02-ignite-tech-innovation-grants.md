## 📋 Program Overview

Ignite's Technology Innovation Grants support cutting-edge technology projects with high potential for commercialization, export earnings, and job creation. The program focuses on emerging technologies including AI, blockchain, IoT, fintech, and advanced data systems.

### **Key Features:**
- Direct grants for R&D and commercialization
- Support for National Incubation Centers (NICs)
- Access to mentorship and training programs
- Connection to investor networks
- Participation in national and international tech events

---

## $ Funding Details

### **Grant Amounts:**
- **Standard Grant:** PKR 5,000,000 (~$17,500 USD)
- **Large Grant:** PKR 10,000,000 (~$35,000 USD)
- **Mega Projects:** PKR 15,000,000+ (~$52,500 USD) - Special approval required

### **Funding Structure:**
- **Milestone-based disbursement** (typically 3-4 tranches)
- **60-70% for product development**, 20-30% for market validation, 10% for operations
- **6-18 month** project duration
- **Quarterly reporting** required

### **Cost Sharing:**
- **Standard Projects:** 100% grant funded
- **Large Projects:** May require 10-20% co-contribution
- **Mega Projects:** 30-40% co-contribution expected

## 📝 Application Process

### **Phase 1: Concept Note (2 pages)**
- Project title and summary
- Technology innovation description
- Market opportunity and competitive advantage
- Team composition
- Budget estimate (high-level)
- Expected outcomes

**Submission:** Online portal at ignite.org.pk  
**Review Time:** 2-3 weeks  
**Shortlist Rate:** ~30%

### **Phase 2: Full Proposal (15-20 pages)**
*Invited applicants only*

- Executive summary
- Detailed technical description
- Work plan and milestones
- Budget breakdown (detailed)
- Team CVs and track record
- Market analysis and go-to-market strategy
- IP strategy
- Risk assessment and mitigation

**Submission:** Within 3 weeks of invitation  
**Review Time:** 4-6 weeks  
**Approval Rate:** ~40% of shortlisted

### **Phase 3: Due Diligence & Contracting**
- Technical evaluation by expert panel
- Financial due diligence
- Team interviews (if required)
- Site visit (for large grants)
- Contract negotiation and signing

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks  
**Success Rate:** ~90% of recommended proposals
