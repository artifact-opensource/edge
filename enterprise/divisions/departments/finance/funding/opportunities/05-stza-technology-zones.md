## 📋 Program Overview

The Special Technology Zones Authority (STZA) was established under the Special Technology Zones Act 2021 to create world-class technology parks across Pakistan, offering unprecedented tax incentives and regulatory facilitation. STZs are modeled on successful international tech zones (like Dubai Internet City, Bangalore IT Parks, Shenzhen SEZ) and provide the most comprehensive tax and business benefits available to technology companies in Pakistan.

Companies registered as "Zone Enterprises" in STZs receive 10-year tax holidays, customs/sales tax exemptions, one-stop regulatory services, and premium infrastructure - creating a Silicon Valley-like ecosystem within Pakistan.

### **Key Features:**
- **10-Year Tax Holiday:** Complete income tax exemption for Zone Enterprises
- **Zero Import Duties:** No customs/sales tax on technology equipment
- **One-Stop Shop:** All regulatory approvals through single STZA window
- **World-Class Infrastructure:** Fiber connectivity, power backup, security
- **Special Labour Laws:** Flexible employment regulations for tech sector
- **Foreign Ownership:** 100% foreign ownership allowed
- **Capital Gains Exemption:** No tax on sale of shares/assets
- **Permanent Establishment:** Act provides long-term policy stability

## $ Funding Details & Financial Benefits

### **1. Income Tax Exemption - 10 Years**

#### **Complete Tax Holiday for Zone Enterprises**
- **Duration:** 10 years from date of Zone Enterprise registration
- **Coverage:** 100% exemption from federal income tax
- **Comparison to Standard:** Normal corporate tax is 29-35%

**Financial Impact Calculation:**

| Revenue Scenario | Annual Tax (Normal) | Annual Tax (STZA) | Annual Savings |
|------------------|---------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| $1M exports | ~$210K (30% of profit) | $0 | **$210K** |
| $3M exports | ~$630K (30% of profit) | $0 | **$630K** |
| $5M exports | ~$1.05M (30% of profit) | $0 | **$1.05M** |

**10-Year Total Savings (at $3M annual exports):** ~$6.3 Million $

**! Important Notes:**
- Exemption applies to income derived from Zone Enterprise activities
- Must maintain operations within designated STZ premises
- Annual compliance reporting required to maintain status
- Post-10-year, reverts to standard tax regime (or renewals possible)

### **2. Customs & Sales Tax Exemptions**

#### **Zero Import Duties**
- **Coverage:** All technology equipment, machinery, components
- **Eligible Items:**
  - Servers, networking equipment, storage systems
  - Development workstations and laptops
  - Software licenses and cloud subscriptions
  - Lab equipment and testing tools
  - Office furniture and fixtures
  - Raw materials for tech manufacturing

**Typical Savings:**
- Import duty: 11-20% (depending on HS code)
- Sales tax: 17%
- Regulatory duty: 0-10%
- **Total import cost savings:** 28-47%

### **3. Capital Gains Tax Exemption**

#### **Zero Tax on Investment Exits**
- Sale of Zone Enterprise shares: 0% capital gains tax

## 📝 Registration Process

### **Step-by-Step Zone Enterprise Registration (8-12 Weeks)**

#### **Phase 1: Preliminary Assessment & STZ Selection (Weeks 1-2)**

**Activities:**
1. Research available STZs and facilities
2. Visit preferred STZ locations (NSTP Islamabad, ASTP Lahore)
3. Assess office space requirements (data center + office: 2,000-3,000 sq.ft)
4. Get rental quotations from STZ management
5. Review infrastructure specs (power, cooling, connectivity)
6. Decide on preferred STZ

**For Artifact Virtual:**
- **Recommended:** NSTP Islamabad (proximity to government, policy, funding)
- **Alternative:** ASTP Lahore (larger talent pool)
- **Space Requirement:** 2,500 sq.ft (office: 1,500 sq.ft, server room: 1,000 sq.ft)

#### **Phase 2: Office Lease & Initial Setup (Weeks 3-5)**

**Activities:**
1. Negotiate lease terms with STZ management
2. Sign lease agreement (typically 3-5 year terms)
3. Pay security deposit (2-3 months rent)
4. Begin office fitout (if needed)
5. Order internet/power connections

**Costs (NSTP Islamabad Example):**
- Rent: PKR 100/sq.ft × 2,500 sq.ft = PKR 250K/month (~$880/month)
- Security deposit: PKR 750K (~$2,640)
- Fitout: PKR 1-2M (~$3,500-$7,000) for server room
- **Total Initial:** ~$13K-$18K

#### **Phase 3: STZA Application Preparation (Weeks 4-6)**

**Required Documents:**

1. **Company Documents:**
   - [ ] Certificate of Incorporation (SECP)
   - [ ] Memorandum & Articles of Association
   - [ ] Form 29 (Directors/Shareholders)
   - [ ] Board Resolution for Zone Enterprise application

2. **Business Plan:**
   - [ ] Comprehensive business plan (15-20 pages)
   - [ ] Technology/product description (HEKTOR details)
   - [ ] Market analysis and export strategy
   - [ ] 3-year financial projections
   - [ ] Employment plan (hiring timeline)

3. **Financial Documents:**
   - [ ] NTN Certificate and FBR registration
   - [ ] Last 2 years tax returns (if applicable)
   - [ ] Bank account certificate
   - [ ] Investment plan and funding sources

4. **STZ-Specific Documents:**
   - [ ] Signed lease agreement from STZ
   - [ ] Office layout plan
   - [ ] Equipment import plan (servers, IT gear)
   - [ ] Employment schedule (10+ employees in 3 years)

5. **Technical Documents:**
   - [ ] Product/technology overview
   - [ ] R&D roadmap
   - [ ] Intellectual property documentation
   - [ ] Export contracts or LOIs

#### **Phase 4: Application Submission (Week 7)**

**Submission Process:**
1. Complete STZA online application form
2. Upload all required documents
3. Pay application fee: PKR 50,000 (~$175)
4. Submit to STZA One-Window portal
5. Receive acknowledgment and tracking number

**Application Portal:** https://portal.stza.gov.pk

#### **Phase 5: STZA Review & Due Diligence (Weeks 8-10)**

**STZA Process:**
1. Initial screening (completeness check)
2. Technical evaluation committee review
3. Financial viability assessment
4. Site visit to proposed premises
5. Background checks (FBR, SECP, security clearance)
6. Sector alignment verification
7. Investment/employment commitment review

**Site Visit Preparation:**
- Have office set up (at least partially)
- Team members present
- HEKTOR demo environment ready
- Presentation about Artifact Virtual (10-15 slides)

#### **Phase 6: STZA Board Approval (Weeks 11-12)**

**Approval Process:**
1. Application forwarded to STZA Board
2. Board meeting (monthly, typically mid-month)
3. Board deliberation and vote
4. Approval notification (via email/portal)
5. Zone Enterprise Certificate issued

**Approval Criteria:**
- Sector eligibility ✓
- Investment commitment ✓
- Employment commitment ✓
- Financial viability ✓
- Tech innovation level ✓
- Export potential ✓

#### **Phase 7: Benefits Activation (Weeks 13-14)**

**Post-Approval Steps:**
1. Receive Zone Enterprise Certificate
2. Register with FBR for tax exemption status
3. Apply for customs exemption certificate
4. Open dedicated bank accounts (if required)
5. Import equipment duty-free (if applicable)
6. Begin quarterly reporting to STZA

**Tax Exemption Setup:**
- File exemption certificate with FBR
- Update company tax profile
- No withholding tax on invoices
- Zero income tax filing (but reporting still required)
