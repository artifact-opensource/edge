## Program Overview

### Vision & Mission

The National AI Fund (NAIF) is Pakistan's flagship financing mechanism for AI research, innovation, and commercialization. Established under the National AI Policy 2025 and overseen by the National AI Council (chaired by the Federal IT Minister), NAIF aims to:

- **Position Pakistan as a regional AI leader** by 2030
- **Train 1 million AI professionals** by 2030
- **Award 3,000 AI scholarships annually** for postgraduate studies
- **Support AI infrastructure development** (compute, data centers, platforms)
- **Drive AI adoption** across education, healthcare, agriculture, governance
- **Foster ethical, human-centric AI** with regulatory sandboxes

### Fund Structure

| **Component** | **Details** |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Primary Source** | Minimum 30% of Ignite National Technology Fund |
| **Estimated Annual** | PKR 300-500 million (~$1-1.8M USD) |
| **Additional Sources** | Public-private partnerships, international collaborations |
| **Beneficiaries** | Universities, startups, industry, IT spin-offs |
| **Sectors** | Healthcare, education, agriculture, governance, energy, manufacturing |
| **Timeline** | Launched Q1 2026, details emerging through Q3 2026 |

### Governance & Oversight

**National AI Council:**
- **Chair:** Federal Minister of IT & Telecom
- **Members:** Provincial ministers, academia, industry leaders, civil society
- **Role:** Strategic direction, resource allocation, policy oversight
- **Support:** Policy Implementation Cell, Master Action Matrix

**Delivery Mechanism:**
- Ignite National Technology Fund (administrative body)
- Centers of Excellence (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore + regional branches)
- Regulatory sandboxes for experimentation
- Public-private partnership models
