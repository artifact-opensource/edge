## 📋 Program Overview

The Pakistan Startup Fund (PSF) is a flagship government initiative designed to de-risk early-stage technology investments and catalyze private capital inflow into Pakistani startups. Unlike traditional VC funds, PSF provides **equity-free, non-dilutive grants** to complement private investment rounds.

### **Key Features:**
- **Equity-Free:** No ownership stake taken by the government
- **Last Cheque Model:** PSF contributes after private investors commit
- **Co-Investment:** Matches 10-30% of total investment round
- **Non-Dilutive:** Founders retain full control and equity
- **Export-Oriented Focus:** Prioritizes scalable, globally competitive ventures

## $ Funding Details

### **Grant Size:**
- **Minimum:** $50,000 USD
- **Maximum:** $1,000,000 USD
- **Typical Range:** $100,000-$300,000 per startup

### **Funding Structure:**
- PSF provides 10-30% of total investment round
- Requires 70%+ to be secured from private investors/VCs first
- Funds released after verification of private investment
- One-time grant per startup (no subsequent rounds eligible)

### **Use of Funds:**
- Product development and R&D
- Market expansion and customer acquisition
- Team hiring and capacity building
- Technology infrastructure
- Working capital for growth

### **Financial Terms:**
- ✓ **Zero equity** taken
- ✓ **No board seats** required
- ✓ **No repayment** obligation
- ✓ **No revenue sharing**
- ✓ **Full control** retained by founders

## ✓ Eligibility Criteria

### **Company Requirements:**
✓ **Incorporated in Pakistan** (registered with SECP)  
✓ **Maximum 10 years** since incorporation  
✓ **Tax Compliant** - Active FBR status, up-to-date returns  
✓ **Innovation-Driven** - Technology-based business model  
✓ **Scalable** - Potential for significant growth and job creation  
✓ **Export Potential** - Aligns with national IT export goals

### **Investment Requirements:**
✓ Must have **secured 70%+ of round** from private investors  
✓ Private investment from **registered VC/angel investors**  
✓ **Term sheet or commitment letter** from lead investor  
✓ **Investment agreement** signed with private investors  
✓ **Bank statements** showing pending/escrowed funds

### **Exclusions:**
✗ Previously received PSF funding  
✗ Non-technology businesses (trading, manufacturing, real estate)  
✗ Single-person operations without growth potential  
✗ Companies with undisclosed legal/tax issues  
✗ Businesses primarily serving local/non-scalable markets

## 📝 Application Process

### **Step 1: Secure Private Investment (Weeks 1-8)**
1. Prepare pitch deck and financial projections
2. Identify and approach suitable VC/angel investors
3. Conduct due diligence and term sheet negotiations
4. Secure commitment for 70%+ of target round
5. Obtain signed term sheet or commitment letter

### **Step 2: Prepare PSF Application (Weeks 9-10)**
1. Complete online application form at ignite.org.pk
2. Gather required documentation (see below)
3. Prepare detailed business plan and use of funds
4. Create video pitch (optional but recommended)
5. Submit application through RGMS portal

### **Step 3: PSF Review & Due Diligence (Weeks 11-14)**
1. Initial screening by Ignite team (2-3 weeks)
2. Technical evaluation of innovation potential
3. Financial due diligence and verification
4. Investor commitment validation
5. Background checks and compliance verification

### **Step 4: Committee Review & Approval (Weeks 15-16)**
1. Presentation to PSF Investment Committee
2. Q&A session with founders (if required)
3. Committee deliberation and vote
4. Formal approval notification

### **Step 5: Disbursement (Weeks 17-18)**
1. Sign grant agreement with Ignite
2. Set up dedicated bank account
3. Submit disbursement request with investor proof of funds
4. Receive PSF grant (typically within 2 weeks)
5. Begin quarterly reporting
