## 📋 Program Overview

The UNESCO Participation Programme is a biennial funding mechanism that provides financial support to UNESCO Member States for projects aligned with UNESCO's strategic priorities. Established in 1978, the programme supports grassroots initiatives in education, science, culture, communication, and social/human sciences.

### **Key Features:**
- **Direct Government Submission:** Applications submitted through national governments (PNCU in Pakistan)
- **Biennial Cycle:** 2026-2027 biennium now accepting applications
- **Non-Reimbursable:** Grants, not loans
- **Category-Based:** Three funding tiers (Emergency, National, Subregional)
- **Quick Turnaround:** 4-6 months from submission to approval

1. Develop detailed project concept (5-7 pages)
2. Define objectives, activities, and deliverables
3. Create budget breakdown
4. Identify beneficiaries and impact metrics
5. Prepare letters of support from partner institutions

#### **Step 3: PNCU Submission (Week 5)**
1. Submit project proposal to PNCU via official channels
2. Include all required documentation
3. Attend PNCU review meeting (if required)
4. Address PNCU feedback and revisions
5. PNCU endorses and forwards to UNESCO Paris

**PNCU Contact:**
- **Address:** Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training, Islamabad
- **Email:** pncu@mofept.gov.pk
- **Phone:** +92-51-9202650
- **Deadline for PNCU Submission:** February 5, 2026 (10 days before UNESCO deadline)

#### **Step 4: UNESCO Review (Weeks 6-16)**
1. UNESCO Secretariat receives applications (Feb 15 deadline)
2. Technical review by UNESCO programme specialists
3. Evaluation against criteria and priorities
4. Budget feasibility assessment
5. Comparative ranking of all applications

#### **Step 5: Approval & Contracting (Weeks 17-20)**
1. UNESCO Executive Board reviews recommendations
2. Formal approval notification to Member State
3. Contract negotiation and signing
4. Bank details and disbursement setup
5. Initial funds disbursement (typically 70% upfront)

#### **Step 6: Implementation (Months 6-18)**
1. Execute project activities per approved plan
2. Submit quarterly progress reports to PNCU/UNESCO
3. Manage budget and procurement per UNESCO rules
4. Document outcomes and impact
5. Submit final report and financial statement
