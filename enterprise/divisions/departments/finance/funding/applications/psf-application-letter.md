Artifact Virtual is developing **HEKTOR**, a breakthrough vector database technology that introduces spectral and perceptual similarity search - a world-first capability not available in competing solutions like Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, or Milvus. While existing vector databases rely solely on semantic embeddings, HEKTOR adds spectral analysis (hyperspectral imaging support) and perceptual similarity, enabling transformative applications in:

- **Medical Imaging:** Advanced diagnostic support through perceptual pattern recognition
- **Agricultural Technology (AgTech):** Crop health monitoring via hyperspectral satellite imagery
- **Remote Sensing:** Climate monitoring, resource mapping, environmental surveillance

Our technology addresses a **$100+ billion global market opportunity** while positioning Pakistan as a leading hub for AI infrastructure innovation.
