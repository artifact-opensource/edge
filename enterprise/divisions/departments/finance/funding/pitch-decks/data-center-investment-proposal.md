## Phase 1 Technical Specifications

### Tier III Data Center (1 MW Capacity)

**Facility:**
- **Size:** 10,000 sq ft (data hall + support areas)
- **Racks:** 100 racks (42U each, 80% utilization target)
- **Power Density:** 10 kW/rack average (AI workloads: 15-20 kW/rack)
- **Total IT Load:** 1 MW (1,000 kW)
- **PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness):** 1.5 (target: 66% efficiency)

**Power Infrastructure:**
- Dual utility feeds (2 x 1.5 MW, N+1 redundancy)
- UPS systems: 1.2 MW (N+1 configuration, 10-15 minute runtime)
- Diesel generators: 2 x 1 MW (N+1, 48-hour fuel capacity)
- PDUs (Power Distribution Units): Intelligent, remote-monitored

**Cooling:**
- Precision air conditioning (CRAC units): 600 kW capacity (N+1)
- Hot aisle/cold aisle containment
- Target: 22-24°C operating temperature

**Network:**
- 10 Gbps uplink (dual ISPs: PTCL, Transworld)
- Dark fiber option (future-proof for 100 Gbps)
- BGP routing, DDoS protection

**Security & Compliance:**
- 24/7 security (guards, biometric access)
- CCTV, motion sensors
- Fire suppression (FM-200 gas, water-based backup)
- Tier III certification (Uptime Institute) - target Q4 2027

**Uptime SLA:** 99.982% (1.6 hours downtime/year)

## Risk Analysis

### Market Risks (Medium)

**Risk:** Pakistan economy volatility (currency devaluation, inflation)

**Mitigation:**
- ✓ Export revenue (USD billing for international HEKTOR customers)
- ✓ STZA forex accounts (protect against PKR devaluation)
- ✓ Long-term customer contracts (3-5 year commitments reduce churn)

---

### Execution Risks (Medium)

**Risk:** Construction delays, cost overruns

**Mitigation:**
- ✓ ADB Technical Assistance Grant (feasibility study de-risks site selection)
- ✓ Fixed-price construction contracts (with penalties for delays)
- ✓ Phased build-out (1 MW initially → 2 MW → 5 MW as demand scales)

---

### Operational Risks (Low-Medium)

**Risk:** Power outages (Pakistan grid reliability issues)

**Mitigation:**
- ✓ Islamabad site (best grid uptime: 99.5% vs. Karachi 98%)
- ✓ N+1 generator backup (48-hour diesel fuel)
- ✓ UPS systems (bridge short outages)

**Historical Data:** Islamabad data centers achieve 99.95%+ uptime (Tier III standard)

---

### Competitive Risks (Medium)

**Risk:** Existing data center operators (PTCL, Multinet, Supernet) compete

**Mitigation:**
- ✓ Differentiation: AI-optimized facility (GPU, high-density racks) vs. general-purpose
- ✓ HEKTOR lock-in: Co-location customers use HEKTOR vector DB (switching costs)
- ✓ STZA tax advantages (10-15% lower prices than non-STZA competitors)

**Overall Risk Level:** Medium (manageable with mitigations)
