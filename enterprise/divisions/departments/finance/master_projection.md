The services arm provides stable cash flow and customer relationships that feed product upsells.

## VI. Product-Service Synergy Matrix

Products and services are designed to create cross-sell flywheel effects:

| Service Entry Point | Product Upsell | Revenue Multiplier |
|---------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| VPS Customer → | HEKTOR Cloud, GLADIUS API | 3-5x MRR uplift |
| Managed Hosting → | Artifact ERP, AVPM | 5-10x MRR uplift |
| Consulting Engagement → | Enterprise License (any product) | 10-20x project-to-license |
| Colocation Customer → | On-prem deployment + support | 2-3x MRR uplift |
| Domain Registration → | Hosting bundle + VPS | 5-8x MRR uplift |
| ERP Customer → | HEKTOR analytics, METEOR | 2-4x MRR uplift |

**Estimated Flywheel Impact:** 20-30% of service customers convert to product customers within 12 months, contributing 40-50% of total product revenue by Y3.
