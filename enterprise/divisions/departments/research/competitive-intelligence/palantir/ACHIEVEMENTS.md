## 🏆 Major Business Milestones

### Company Formation and Early Years (2003-2010)

**2003: Company Founded**
- Founded by Peter Thiel, Nathan Gettings, Joe Lonsdale, Stephen Cohen
- Initial vision: Counter-terrorism data analytics
- Seed funding from founders
- **Significance:** PayPal mafia roots, strong founding team

**2004: First Government Contracts**
- Initial pilot programs with intelligence agencies
- Technology validation in real-world scenarios
- **Significance:** Early product-market fit in government

**2005: In-Q-Tel Investment**
- CIA venture capital arm invests in Palantir
- Validates technology for intelligence community
- Opens doors to classified programs
- **Significance:** Strategic validation and market access

**2008: Palantir Gotham Launch**
- First major product release
- Focused on defense and intelligence
- Battle-tested technology
- **Significance:** Establishes market position

**2010: $90M Funding Round**
- Valuation reaches $725M
- Investors include Founders Fund, In-Q-Tel
- **Significance:** Major validation, growth capital
