## ◉ Executive Summary

### Who is Palantir?

Palantir Technologies Inc. is a public American software company that specializes in big data analytics and artificial intelligence. Founded in 2003 by Peter Thiel, Nathan Gettings, Joe Lonsdale, Stephen Cohen, and Alex Karp, Palantir has become one of the most influential and controversial data analytics companies globally.

**Key Facts (as of February 2026):**
- **Founded:** 2003
- **IPO:** September 2020 (NYSE: PLTR)
- **Headquarters:** Denver, Colorado, USA
- **CEO:** Alex Karp
- **Market Cap:** ~$40-50B (varies)
- **Revenue (2025):** ~$2.5-3B estimated
- **Employees:** ~4,000-5,000
- **Primary Markets:** Government, Defense, Intelligence, Healthcare, Financial Services, Energy

## ■ Competitive Positioning

### Palantir's Strengths
✓ Strong government and defense relationships  
✓ Proven track record in complex data integration  
✓ Robust security and compliance capabilities  
✓ High-value enterprise contracts  
✓ Advanced ontology-based data modeling  
✓ Strong brand recognition in target markets  

### Palantir's Weaknesses
! High cost and complexity  
! Long implementation timelines  
! Perceived as "black box" by some customers  
! Limited openness and interoperability  
! Controversial reputation (privacy concerns)  
! Heavy sales and implementation overhead  

### Our Differentiation Opportunities
◉ More cost-effective solutions  
◉ Faster time to value  
◉ Greater transparency and explainability  
◉ Better developer experience  
◉ More flexible deployment options  
◉ Focus on specific verticals
