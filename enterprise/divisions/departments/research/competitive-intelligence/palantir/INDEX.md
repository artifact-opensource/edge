### Ethical Guidelines

**What We Do:**
✓ Use only publicly available information

✓ Cite sources appropriately
✓ Follow legal and regulatory requirements

## ■ Information Validation

### Verification Standards

**Tier 1 Information (Highest Confidence):**
- Official company disclosures (SEC filings, press releases)
- Government contracts and records
- Patent filings
- Direct observations from public events

**Tier 2 Information (High Confidence):**
- Reputable news sources with primary sourcing
- Industry analyst reports from major firms
- Customer case studies and testimonials
- Conference presentations

**Tier 3 Information (Medium Confidence):**
- Single-source news reports
- Social media from verified accounts
- Job postings and organizational charts
- Industry rumors with partial confirmation

**Tier 4 Information (Noted but Unverified):**
- Anonymous sources
- Unverified social media
- Speculative analysis
- Industry gossip

**Classification in Documents:**
- Facts are cited with sources
- Analysis is clearly labeled as interpretation
- Speculation is explicitly noted
- Confidence levels indicated when appropriate
