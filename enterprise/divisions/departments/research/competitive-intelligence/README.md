## ▫ Research Standards

### Information Sources

**Public Sources Only:**
- SEC filings and investor relations
- Press releases and announcements
- Government contract databases
- Patent filings
- News and industry coverage
- Conference presentations
- Customer case studies

**Prohibited Sources:**
- Insider information
- Confidential or leaked documents
- Unauthorized access to systems
- Social engineering
- Competitive espionage

### Ethical Guidelines

✓ **What We Do:**
- Gather publicly available information
- Analyze and synthesize intelligence
- Provide strategic insights
- Respect intellectual property
- Follow all applicable laws

❌ **What We Don't Do:**
- Industrial espionage
- Misrepresentation
- Unauthorized access
- Breach of confidentiality
- Anticompetitive practices
