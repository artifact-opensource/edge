## 🔍 Current Priority: Palantir Technologies

### Why Palantir?

Palantir Technologies represents our most significant competitive challenge and learning opportunity. As a leader in data integration, analytics, and AI platforms serving government and enterprise customers, understanding Palantir's capabilities, strategies, and weaknesses is critical for Artifact Virtual's positioning.

### Research Objectives

1. **Comprehensive Capability Mapping**
   - Document all Palantir products and services
   - Analyze technical architecture and stack
   - Assess AI/ML capabilities
   - Evaluate integration capabilities

2. **Strategic Intelligence**
   - Track ongoing projects (public and private where disclosed)
   - Monitor customer wins and losses
   - Analyze pricing and business model
   - Identify strategic partnerships

3. **Competitive Positioning**
   - Identify capability gaps vs. Palantir
   - Discover weaknesses and limitations
   - Find differentiation opportunities
   - Develop counter-positioning strategies

4. **Technology Learning**
   - Study successful implementations
   - Analyze technical approaches
   - Identify best practices to adopt
   - Learn from their innovations

**Research Location:** [`competitive-intelligence/palantir/`](competitive-intelligence/palantir/)
