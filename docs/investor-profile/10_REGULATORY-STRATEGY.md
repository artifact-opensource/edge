## PSEB (TechDestination) Registration

### Overview
The Pakistan Software Export Board, now rebranded and operating via the **TechDestination** portal (techdestination.com), is the primary regulatory body for IT and IT-enabled services companies in Pakistan. Registration provides:

- Official recognition as an IT/ITeS company
- Tax exemptions on IT export income (100% exempt under Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Clause 133)
- Access to Software Technology Parks (43 nationwide)
- Inclusion in PSEB's company directory (17,000+ registered companies)
- Eligibility for government incentive schemes
- Credibility for international business development

### Registration Process

| Step | Action | Timeline | Cost |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| 1 | Create TechDestination portal account | 1 day | Free |
| 2 | Submit company documentation (SECP certificate, NTN, bank letter) | 1 day | Free |
| 3 | Complete online registration form | 1 day | Free |
| 4 | Pay membership fee | 1 day | PKR 5,000-30,000 |
| 5 | PSEB review and approval | 5-15 business days | — |
| 6 | Receive PSEB registration certificate | 1-2 days post-approval | — |
| **Total** | | **10-20 business days** | **PKR 5,000-30,000** |

### Membership Fee Structure

| Category | Annual Fee (PKR) | Criteria |
|----------|-----------------|----------|
| Startup (new registration) | 5,000 | Company <2 years old, <PKR 10M revenue |
| Small | 10,000 | Company 2-5 years, PKR 10-50M revenue |
| Medium | 20,000 | Company 5-10 years, PKR 50-200M revenue |
| Large | 30,000 | Company >10 years or >PKR 200M revenue |

**AV Status:** Startup category — PKR 5,000 annual fee (~$18 USD)

### Tax Benefits Post-Registration

| Benefit | Description | Authority |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| IT Export Income Tax Exemption | 100% exempt from income tax on IT/ITeS export revenue | FBR / Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Cl. 133 |
| Reduced WHT on IT Services | Reduced withholding tax rates for registered IT companies | FBR |
| Software Technology Park Access | Subsidized office space, shared infrastructure | PSEB |
| R&D Tax Credit | Potential R&D expenditure deductions | FBR |

## PTA Licensing Strategy

### Overview
The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) regulates all telecommunications, Internet, hosting, VPN, and domain services in Pakistan. AV requires PTA licensing for:
1. **VPS and Managed Web Hosting** — requires CVAS registration
2. **VPN Services** — requires IP/VPN registration
3. **Domain Name Registration** — requires PKNIC reseller accreditation (administered by PKNIC, not PTA)
4. **Larger Networking** — may require ISP or LDI licenses depending on scope

### PTA License Categories (Relevant to AV)

| License | Full Name | Purpose | Fee (est.) | Timeline |
|---------|-----------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **CVAS** | Class Value Added Services | Web hosting, VPS, cloud services, content delivery | PKR 100K-500K | 30-90 days |
| **IP/VPN** | Internet Protocol / Virtual Private Network | VPN services for enterprise clients | PKR 200K-1M | 60-120 days |
| **ISP** | Internet Service Provider | If AV provides Internet access services | PKR 1M-5M | 90-180 days |
| **LDI** | Long Distance International | International data/voice services (future) | PKR 10M+ | 180+ days |
| **TIP** | Telecom Infrastructure Provider | If AV builds physical infrastructure (future) | PKR 5M+ | 180+ days |
| **Type Approval** | Equipment Type Approval | Required for deploying networking equipment | Per equipment | 30-60 days |
| **IoT LPWAN** | IoT Low Power WAN License | If AV enters IoT infrastructure (future) | PKR 500K-2M | 90-120 days |

### CVAS Registration (Priority 1)

**Required for:** VPS hosting, managed web hosting, cloud platform, CDN services

**Process:**
1. Submit application via PTA OASIS portal (oasis.pta.gov.pk)
2. Provide company documentation (SECP certificate, NTN, financials)
3. Technical compliance documentation (network diagram, security measures)
4. PTA review — technical and financial evaluation
5. Compliance with CTDISR (Communications Terms, Definitions, and Inspection Standard Requirements)
6. License issuance

**Key Requirements:**
- SECP-registered company (AV: completed, #0325693)
- National Tax Number (NTN)
- Bank guarantee or financial security deposit
- Technical infrastructure documentation
- Security and lawful intercept compliance (CTDISR)
- Data center / hosting infrastructure details

### IP/VPN Registration (Priority 2)

**Required for:** Enterprise VPN services for clients, secure data tunneling

**Process:** Similar to CVAS — submitted via PTA OASIS portal with additional security documentation around encryption, key management, and lawful intercept capability.

### CTDISR Compliance

All PTA-licensed services must comply with the **Communications Terms, Definitions, and Inspection Standard Requirements**:

| Requirement | Description | AV Readiness |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Lawful Intercept | Capability to provide data to LEAs on court order | To be implemented |
| Data Retention | Retain communication metadata for specified period | Database architecture supports this |
| Network Security | Minimum security standards for network infrastructure | SOC 2 roadmap aligns |
| User Registration | Customer KYC for service subscribers | ERP/CRM integration planned |
| Content Filtering | Compliance with Pakistan content regulations | Web filtering capability to be added |
| Reporting | Regular reporting to PTA on service metrics | Automated reporting planned |

## STZA — Special Technology Zones Authority

### Overview
STZA was established under the STZA Act 2021 to create technology zones with preferential tax and regulatory treatment. Benefits include:

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Corporate Tax Holiday | Up to 10 years exemption from corporate income tax |
| Sales Tax Exemption | Exempt from sales tax on services within STZA zones |
| Customs Duty Waiver | Duty-free import of equipment, hardware, furniture |
| Withholding Tax Reduction | Reduced or eliminated withholding taxes |
| Labor Law Flexibility | Flexible employment regulations |
| One-Stop Regulatory | Single window for all approvals |
| Foreign Equity | 100% foreign ownership permitted |

### Current Status (February 2026)
**Caution:** New STZA zone notifications are currently **paused** due to IMF Extended Fund Facility (EFF) commitments. The IMF has raised concerns about the fiscal impact of blanket tax exemptions.

**Existing Operational Zones:**
- Islamabad (operational)
- Lahore (operational)
- Karachi (under development)

**AV Position:** Monitor STZA developments. If new zone notifications resume, apply for STZA Zone Enterprise status in Islamabad zone. Do not depend on STZA benefits in financial projections until status is confirmed.
