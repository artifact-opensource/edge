## 10. The Pakistan Advantage

Pakistan is positioning itself as a **global compute hub** through unprecedented policy initiatives:

### Strategic Factors

| Factor | Advantage |
|--------|-----------|
| **2,000 MW Power Allocation** | Government dedicating surplus electricity for AI/crypto at ~$0.04-0.086/kWh |
| **CPEC Fiber Corridor** | 4 Tbps terrestrial link to China; low-latency to Asia |
| **PEACE Cable** | High-bandwidth submarine to Africa/Europe |
| **National AI Fund** | $1M+ programs launching 2026-2027 |
| **STZA Zones** | 10-year tax holiday, duty-free imports |
| **IT Export Boom** | $3B in 2024 (+28% YoY); targeting rapid expansion |
