# CEO WEEKLY OPERATING SYSTEM
================================================

**Document Classification:** Executive - Operational  
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-08  
**Owner:** Office of the CEO  
**Purpose:** Dynamic weekly management system to prevent overwhelm and ensure departmental alignment

---

## PHILOSOPHY

This Weekly Operating System replaces scattered daily checklists with a **focused departmental cadence**. Each day has a primary departmental focus, reducing context switching and creating predictable touchpoints for all teams.

**Core Principles:**
1. **One Department Per Day** - Deep focus beats shallow scanning
2. **Weekend Synthesis** - Weekly reflection and next-week planning
3. **Dynamic Adaptation** - Adjust priorities based on real-time needs
4. **Reduced Documentation** - Single source of truth for weekly operations

---

## DYNAMIC ADAPTATION PROTOCOL

### When to Adjust the Weekly Plan

**Triggers for Schedule Changes:**
- Critical incidents or emergencies
- Major customer issues
- Significant market events
- Unexpected opportunities
- Team crises

**Adaptation Rules:**
1. **Preserve Department Days When Possible** - Move meetings, don't cancel department focus
2. **Protect Saturday Synthesis** - This is your strategic anchor; only skip for true emergencies
3. **Adjust, Don't Abandon** - Shift the plan, don't throw it away
4. **Document Changes** - Note why you adapted in `notes.md`
5. **Return to Rhythm** - Get back to weekly cadence as soon as possible

**Emergency Override:**
If a critical situation requires full attention:
- [ ] Notify executive team of schedule change
- [ ] Delegate department check-ins to department heads
- [ ] Still do quick Saturday synthesis (even 30 mins)
- [ ] Resume normal cadence next week

## REDUCING OVERWHELM: PRINCIPLES

### 1. Single Focus Days
**Problem:** Context switching drains energy  
**Solution:** One department per day = deep work and clear priorities

### 2. Predictable Rhythms
**Problem:** Reactive scheduling creates chaos  
**Solution:** Team knows when to expect CEO attention

### 3. Weekend Processing
**Problem:** Week flies by without learning  
**Solution:** Structured reflection consolidates lessons

### 4. Dynamic Not Rigid
**Problem:** Strict plans break under pressure  
**Solution:** Adapt the plan, keep the rhythm

### 5. Consolidated Documentation
**Problem:** Too many files to track  
**Solution:** Clear document hierarchy and purpose

## APPENDIX: DAILY TIME BLOCKS (REFERENCE)

For detailed daily time blocks, refer to `sop.md`. This weekly system defines **what** to focus on each day; `sop.md` defines **how** to structure each day.

**Integration Model:**
- **Weekly SOP** (this document) = Strategic calendar and department focus
- **Daily SOP** (`sop.md`) = Tactical time blocks and checklists

All systems work together to create a **scalable, sustainable operating rhythm** for the CEO role.
