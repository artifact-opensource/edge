## PURPOSE

This document defines the **tactical daily time blocks** for the Chief Executive Officer of Artifact Virtual. These procedures ensure consistent daily execution within the strategic weekly cadence.

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This document works in conjunction with `ceo-weekly-sop.md`

**Document Hierarchy:**
1. **`ceo-weekly-sop.md`** ← START HERE (Weekly strategic rhythm and department focus)
2. **`sop.md`** ← THIS DOCUMENT (Daily tactical time blocks)
3. **`todo.md`** (Specific tasks and milestones)
4. **`notes.md`** (Quick capture and decisions)
5. **`objective.md`** (Long-term strategic goals)

**How to Use:**
- Use `ceo-weekly-sop.md` to determine your **departmental focus** for the day
- Use this document for **detailed time blocks** and daily rhythm
- Adapt time blocks based on which department is the focus for that day
